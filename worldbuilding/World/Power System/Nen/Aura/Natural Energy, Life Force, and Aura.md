The world contains three closely related but qualitatively distinct forms of energy: **Natural Energy**, **Life Force**, and **Aura**. They are quantitatively equivalent states of the same underlying energy — conversion between them is always **1:1**, and converting changes the *quality* of the energy, never its *quantity*. Ten units of Natural Energy converted completely into Life Force produce exactly ten Life Force; if that Life Force subsequently becomes Aura, it produces exactly ten Aura. What distinguishes the three is what state the energy exists in, where it can naturally occur, and what it is capable of doing — not how much of it there is.

## Natural Energy

Natural Energy is the form of energy that exists throughout the nonliving environment — present in air, water, earth, and inanimate matter. Unlike Life Force and Aura, it does not require a living organism in order to exist at all.

Plants naturally absorb Natural Energy through their roots and leaves, alongside the ordinary processes by which they take in light, water, and nutrients, and they convert what they absorb into Life Force. Humans and most animals cannot perform that conversion directly; instead they obtain Life Force secondhand, through the food chain — animals eat plants, other animals eat those animals, and so on up the chain. Natural Energy is therefore the environmental reservoir from which all biological Life Force ultimately originates.

## Life Force

Life Force is Natural Energy that has been converted into the living state. Living organisms possess Life Force rather than Natural Energy as the energy of their own biological existence — plants have it, and so does every non-sentient organism.

Life Force is *not* Aura. An organism can hold an enormous quantity of Life Force while possessing exactly zero Aura. The conversion from Natural Energy is quantitative and 1:1: the change is in the energy's nature, not its amount.

## Aura

Aura is the sentient form of Life Force. When a living organism becomes sentient, its Life Force undergoes a qualitative and **irreversible** transformation into Aura.

Sentience here doesn't mean humanlike intelligence — it means the capacity for subjective experience: a creature that feels emotion, responds through genuine internal experience, and makes decisions has what's needed for Aura. Animals can and do possess Aura on this basis. Ordinary plants cannot, but a plant that somehow developed genuine sentience would undergo the same transformation as any other organism, since the transformation applies to whatever Life Force the creature already has.

The distinction is absolute. A non-sentient creature has Life Force greater than zero and Aura equal to zero — full stop; there is no partial or trace Aura to be found in it. A sentient creature has *no* separate reserve of ordinary Life Force sitting beneath its Aura: once the transformation happens, its Life Force **is** its Aura, not a second pool alongside it. This holds for every sentient creature without exception, and because the transformation is purely qualitative, the quantity carries through unchanged.

The complete conversion chain, then, is one continuous quantity passing through three states: Natural Energy becomes Life Force becomes Aura, in equivalent amounts at every step. See [[The Standard Slime]] for how that quantity is actually measured.
