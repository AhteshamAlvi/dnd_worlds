Aura has no special named unit the way mass has kilograms or distance has meters. [[Natural Energy, Life Force, and Aura|Natural Energy, Life Force, and Aura]] instead share a single standardized scale, and that scale is anchored to a physical, reproducible phenomenon: the formation of a **Standard Slime**.

## How a Standard Slime forms

A normal slime forms when Natural Energy accumulates within suitable water until an exact formation threshold is reached: water, plus dissolved impurities, plus enough Natural Energy, coalesces into a slime. The amount of Natural Energy required is invariant — every Standard Slime forms with exactly the same quantity of it. This isn't an average taken across many slimes; formation itself happens at a fixed threshold, which is what makes the phenomenon reproducible at all. Once that threshold is reached the slime coalesces into its standard form, and Standard Slimes consequently share the same formation Natural Energy quantity, the same standard body size and density, and the same quantity of water incorporated during formation.

Completely purified water cannot form a slime, because slime formation consolidates the water's dissolved impurities into the slime's **core**, and purified water has no impurities to consolidate. The particular substances that end up in a given slime's core can affect its traits, capabilities, or strength — but this never changes the quantity of Natural Energy the slime contains. Two Standard Slimes can hold exactly the same amount of Natural Energy while behaving very differently, purely because their cores are made of different material. Slime strength is therefore not a matter of possessing more Natural Energy than another slime; it's a matter of what the core is made of. Rarer slime variants exist that depart from this model entirely, but they are exceptional forms and are never used for measurement.

## The energy standard

The amount of Natural Energy contained in one Standard Slime at the moment of formation defines the reference quantity of the entire energy scale:

> **1 = the Natural Energy contained in one Standard Slime at formation.**

Because Natural Energy, Life Force, and Aura convert at 1:1, one unit on this scale means the same thing in all three states: one Natural Energy quantity, one Life Force quantity, and one Aura quantity are each defined as the equivalent of one Standard Slime's worth of formation energy.

The number itself carries no special unit name. Saying a creature has an Aura Pool of 10 means the total quantity of Aura making up that creature's life force is equivalent to the Natural Energy contained in **ten** Standard Slimes at formation — not that the creature possesses "ten Aura points" or "ten auras." The Standard Slime is the physical reference the number is derived from, nothing more.

## The normal human baseline

A normal, healthy human with ordinary physical attributes possesses a Maximum Aura Pool of approximately **10** — quantitatively equivalent to the Natural Energy in about ten Standard Slimes. This assumes an ordinary adult baseline of roughly CON 10 and VIT 10; see [[Aura Reserves]] for how those two attributes actually determine the pool.

Nen awakening does not create Aura, and does not by itself increase this quantity. An ordinary human who has never awakened Nen still has Aura, because a human is already a sentient organism — Nen only grants conscious access to and control over Aura that was there the whole time. An unawakened human is not an Aura-less human; a normal, unawakened person still carries approximately 10 Aura on this scale, same as anyone else at that baseline.
