**Aura** is the sentient form of Life Force — the energy of a living body once that body is capable of genuine subjective experience. Every sentient creature has it whether or not it has ever touched Nen; Nen is simply the discipline of consciously perceiving and shaping the Aura that was already there. This section lays out where Aura comes from, how it is measured, what a creature's reserve of it actually consists of, and how it relates to Nen as a discipline.
