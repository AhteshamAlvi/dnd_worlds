After acquiring a working command of the basics and intermediate principles, a student explores a series of advanced principles that often combine and/or derive from the Four Major Principles and the intermediate ones. These principles vastly expand the user's options in combat and other uses, but they also consume more aura than the basic and intermediate principles.
