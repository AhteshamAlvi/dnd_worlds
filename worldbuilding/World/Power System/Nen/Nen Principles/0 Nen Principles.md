Fifteen principles in three tiers. The [[0 Four Major Principles|four majors]] are the whole of Nen in miniature — everything above them is a combination, an extension, or a refinement of those four. The [[0 Intermediate Principles|intermediates]] add reach and precision; the [[0 Advanced Principles|advanced]] stack several principles at once and cost accordingly.

Each entry below is a one-line summary. Follow the link for the full page.

## [[0 Four Major Principles|Four Major Principles]]

Learned in this order, and eventually second nature — an experienced user holds Ten even in their sleep.

- **[[Ten]]** ("Envelop") — keeps aura from leaking away, circulating it through and around the body as a thin permanent shroud. The foundation of everything else, and a passive defense that also slows aging.
- **[[Zetsu]]** ("Suppress") — closes the aura nodes entirely, halting all outflow and hiding the user's presence. Sharpens perception of *other* people's aura, and leaves the body completely undefended.
- **[[Ren]]** ("Refine") — outputs far more aura than Ten, projected outward explosively to amplify strength and durability. The offensive counterpart to Ten and the fuel supply for everything above it.
- **[[Hatsu]]** ("Release") — the user's personal expression of Nen, projecting aura to perform a function nobody else's does. This is where a [[Nen Ability]] comes from.

## [[0 Intermediate Principles|Intermediate Principles]]

Combinations and extensions of the four majors. More options, more aura spent.

- **[[Gyō]]** ("Focus") — concentrates a larger share of aura into one body part, most often the eyes. Powers up that part, weakens everywhere else, and is the standard counter to *In*.
- **[[In]]** ("Conceal") — hides aura without stopping it, making it imperceptible to the senses and to Nen perception alike. Built for ambushes and traps; countered by *Gyō* in the eyes.
- **[[En]]** ("Encircle") — extends *Ren* over a wide area and shapes it with *Ten*, usually into a sphere the user can feel everything inside. Minimum 2 m radius held over a minute; mastery reaches 50 m, and it is exhausting the entire time.
- **[[Shū]]** ("Enfold") — extends aura around an object so it functions as an extension of the body. A *Shū*-wrapped weapon is strengthened, protected, and can carry other principles with it.
- **[[Ken]]** ("Fortify") — holds *Ren* continuously, keeping roughly ten times Ten's aura over the whole body at once. Guards every direction evenly, which makes it the best answer to *Kō* and tiring to sustain.
- **[[Chū]]** ("Infuse") — drives aura *inward* instead of outward, reinforcing muscle, bone, and senses from the inside. Enhancers get a much better return on it than anyone else; the hardest intermediate to master.

## [[0 Advanced Principles|Advanced Principles]]

Several principles run at once. Powerful, expensive, and each with a specific way of getting you killed.

- **[[Kō]]** ("Temper") — dumps *all* available aura into one body part or weapon, combining Ten, Zetsu, Ren, Chū, and Gyō. Devastating on that one point and completely undefended everywhere else, so it is treated as a purely offensive principle.
- **[[Ryū]]** ("Flow") — real-time redistribution of aura at any ratio, rather than Kō's all-or-nothing or Ken's even split. Lets a user hurt a *Ken* opponent without opening themselves up like *Kō* does, and demands reading the enemy's aura at a glance.
- **[[Fū]]** ("Enclose") — defines a boundary at the edge of an *En* sphere. The user stops sensing the interior but instantly senses anything crossing the edge, and that edge dampens hostile Nen passing through it — the standard counter to area-of-effect abilities.
- **[[Jū]]** ("Reject") — *Ken* directed inward, with *Hatsu* used to nullify Nen applied to the user's own body. Throws off manipulation and curses, at the price of no external offense or defense while it holds.
- **[[Yū]]** ("Heal") — hyperfocuses *Ren* and *Chū* down to the cellular level with *Gyō*, then uses *Hatsu* to force regeneration, up to entire limbs. Costs more aura than any other principle, cannot be maintained alongside anything else, and cannot restore blood or brain.
