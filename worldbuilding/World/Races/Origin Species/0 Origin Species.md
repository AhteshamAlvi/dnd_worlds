The **origin species** are the three peoples the world actually started with: the [[Ancient Giants]], the [[Devils]], and the [[Angels]]. They are not three creations. Every other humanoid people in the world ([[Humans]] included) descends from them.

Near the end of the **[[Mythological Era]]**, the northern trunk of the **World Tree** rewrote the ecology around the North Pole. Its roots, trunk, and crown flooded the environment with sap, pollen, natural energy, heat, and Aura-rich organic matter on a scale nothing else in history matches.

This did not conjure intelligent beings. What it did was accelerate a social animal that was already living throughout the Tree's ecosystem: the [[Crown Hominid]]. Different populations of that animal ended up isolated in the three vertical bands of the Tree: the ground and lower trunk, the subterranean root network, and the upper canopy. Over an immense span of time they diverged into three separate but closely related species.

Because they came from the same ancestor and kept compatible souls and compatible reproductive structures, they can still produce fertile offspring with one another. That biological closeness is what later makes [[The Origin of Humans|the creation of humanity]] possible.

## Three concentrations of one power

Each species was shaped by the way the Tree's power behaved in its band.

| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Habitat**                | Ground, lower trunk, polar mountains                                                    | Hollow roots and caverns                                                                      | Upper canopy and sky islands                                                           |
| **Primary Trait**          | **Auraframe** — permanent unconscious Aura reinforcement through a mineralized skeleton | **Imposition** — Advanced Aura powered perception allowing detection of spaces and boundaries | **Grace** — ambient natural energy absorbed, filtered, and converted into ordered Aura |

None of the three are Nen-users at this stage. Each simply has a body that solves its own environment, and formal [[0 Nen|Nen]] arrives later to build on what the body was already doing.

## The Beginning of Spirits

[[Spirits|Blessed and Cursed Spirits]] form alongside the rise of true intelligence in the three origin species, but they are not a fourth origin species. They are not part of the Crown Hominid family and they play no biological role in the creation of Humans.

The earliest Spirits appear once Ancient Giants, Devils, and Angels develop language, shared memory, ritual, and emotion directed at common ideas. Individual emotion disperses too easily to form anything; repeated *collective* emotion accumulates. Fear of the underground dark, joy at the return of seasonal light, gratitude toward the World Tree, grief for the dead, terror of the Primordials, pride in one's people, and hope for the survival of children become the first great emotional wells.

Natural energy collects around those wells. Nen gives them form. Repetition gives them identity. Eventually they wake up with wills of their own.

Spirits therefore enter the world at the same historical boundary as civilization itself — not as a people and not as anyone's descendants, but as the first supernatural consequence of sapient communal emotion.
