The middle tier of the family tree. A **secondary species** is a lineage that descended from the three [[0 Origin Species|Origin Species]] — the [[Ancient Giants]], [[Devils]], and [[Angels]] — and is no longer a going concern in the modern world. Two ways to end up here, and only two:

- **Extinct.** The lineage died out, or was killed out, and cannot reproduce itself any longer. A handful of individuals surviving does not undo this: if what remains cannot carry the line into the next generation, the species is finished as a species, however impressive the last of them may be.
- **Precursor.** The lineage did not die so much as *become* something else. It gave rise to one or more of the peoples now filed under [[0 Current Species|Current Species]] and stopped existing in its own right — the transitional shape between an origin species and a modern one.

The line against [[0 Current Species|Current Species]] is population, not prominence. A people down to its last ten or twenty, hidden and hunted, is still current; a people down to one is not.

## Why this tier exists

The [[0 Origin Species|Origin Species]] account has three peoples at the top and several dozen at the bottom, which leaves a great deal of unexplained distance in between. Angels did not become [[Sky People]] in one step, and the Ancient Giants of the polar mountains did not become Elbaf's warriors in one step either. The species that occupied that distance are what this folder is for, and most of them are still unwritten.

The extinct **Ancient Giant** lineage described at the bottom of [[Giant]] — larger again than the true-blooded lines, surviving only as corpses, legends, and the occasional reanimated specimen — is the clearest candidate for a proper entry here.
