An Organization is any body that people *join* rather than are born into: a guild, an army, a school, a corps, a syndicate, a church, a crew, a conspiracy. What separates one from a [[0 Nations|Nation]] is that it holds no territory by right, and from a [[0 Clans|Clan]] that its membership is chosen, not inherited.
