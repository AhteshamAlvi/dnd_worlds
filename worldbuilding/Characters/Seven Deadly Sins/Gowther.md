**Nen Type:** [[Manipulation|Manipulator]]

Invasion reaches into a target's mind and moves what it finds there — memories, perception, belief. Manipulation aimed at minds rather than bodies or objects. Note that in this system, a mind-directed effect is resisted by a **Spirit save (SPI)**, not by ordinary defenses.

**Nen Ability:** Invasion ([[Manipulation]])
- Enters and rewrites the mind: reads memory, alters perception, plants illusions, and rearranges what a person believes happened.

## Invasion

Expressions range from reading a target's memories, to broadcasting an illusion an entire group experiences as real, to editing a specific memory out of someone and leaving the hole behind. The last of those is what makes him genuinely dangerous and genuinely distrusted — he has used it on allies, reasoning about it as a problem to be solved rather than a violation.

## Being a doll

He is a [[0 Artificial Species|Construct]]: built, not born, and originally without real emotion. Much of his arc is the question of whether something assembled can come to actually feel rather than imitate. Mechanically the relevant part is that he does not tire, age, or fear the way a living body does.
