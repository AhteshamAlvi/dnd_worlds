**Race:** [[Goddess]] — reincarnated into the royal line of [[Liones]]

**Nen Type:** [[Transmutation|Transmuter]]
![[transmutation.png]]
Ark converts her aura into a holy light that heals what it touches and burns what is made of demon aura. Changing the *nature* of one's own aura into something with new properties is Transmutation; the fact that it is projected outward makes [[Emission]] her natural adjacent category.

**Nen Ability:** Ark ([[Transmutation]])
- Turns her aura into holy light: it mends the wounded, purges corruption, and is actively caustic to [[Demon]] aura.

## Ark

The same light does two different jobs depending on what it lands on, which is the point — it is one property, not two abilities. Against an ordinary target it is a healing and restorative effect. Against a demon it is a weapon, and a badly asymmetric one: the [[Goddess]] and [[Demon]] clans are built so that each side's basic output is poison to the other.

## Reincarnation

Her defining trait is not the ability. She dies and is reborn, repeatedly, carrying the same soul into a new life — a curse laid on her and Meliodas together. Treated as a standing supernatural condition on the character rather than something she casts.
