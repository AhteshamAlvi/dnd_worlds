**Race:** [[Fairy]] — the Fairy King

**Nen Type:** [[Conjuration|Conjurer]]
![[conjuration.png]]
Chastiefol is a real object brought into being and then re-shaped at will — a conjured weapon with a library of forms, which is Conjuration's signature move. Its origin in the Sacred Tree is lore, not a separate mechanic; what matters mechanically is that he manifests the thing and it obeys the shape he gives it.

**Nen Ability:** Chastiefol ([[Conjuration]])
- A spirit spear cut from the Sacred Tree, conjured at will and reconfigured into a set of distinct named forms.

## Forms

Each form is a different conjured configuration rather than a different ability:
- **Spear** — the base weapon.
- **Guardian** — a massive bear-shaped construct that fights and defends on its own.
- **Fossilization** — splits into countless drifting shards.
- **Increase** — expands into an enormous mass for a single overwhelming blow.
- **Pollen Garden** — a healing and protective field, the defensive expression.

## Flight and levitation

A [[Fairy]] trait rather than an ability. Wings, and the levitation that lets him fight without ever touching the ground, come from the race.
