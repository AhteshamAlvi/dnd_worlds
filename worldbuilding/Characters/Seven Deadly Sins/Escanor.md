**Race:** Human

**Nen Type:** [[Enhancement|Enhancer]]
![[enhancement.png]]
Sunshine is raw output — strength, durability, and heat pushed to absurd magnitudes — with no shaping, no remote effect, and no target-control. That is Enhancement, and it is the cleanest illustration in any verse of why Enhancers hit hardest and do least else.

**Nen Ability:** Sunshine ([[Enhancement]])
- His power rises and falls with the sun's position, peaking at noon and collapsing to nothing at night.

## The Condition

Escanor is the setting's textbook case of a [[Conditions|Condition]] paying for magnitude. The restriction is severe, entirely outside his control, and publicly known — and in exchange the payout at peak is disproportionate to anything a human body should produce. At noon he is arguably the strongest being on the continent; at midnight he is a timid man who cannot fight at all.

Worth noting for design purposes: the Condition is **not** a [[Vows and Limitations|Vow]] he chose. He cannot break it, negotiate it, or spend it early — which is exactly what makes it worth so much.

## The One

The hour of peak power. Distinct enough in behavior and bearing that it reads as a separate person, and treated in-world as such.

## Rhitta

His axe, which stores sunlight during the hours he is weak and releases it as heat. A vessel for the ability rather than an ability of its own.
