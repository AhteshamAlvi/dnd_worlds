[[0 Index|Index]]

The canon roster. One folder per source verse, each holding that verse's notable figures — the raw material behind [[Canon Benchmarks]], which is where a canon character stops being a reference and becomes a set of Nenworld numbers to calibrate against.

These are reference notes, not [[0 Races|Race]] or [[0 Nen|Nen]] lore. A character page records what the figure *is* — their [[0 Types of Nen|Nen type]], their [[Nen Ability]], their tier — so that the rulebook has something concrete to point at when it claims a given band of power is reachable. Most are still stubs waiting on those two fields.
