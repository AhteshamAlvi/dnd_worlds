
|    Lv. | Classification        | Meaning                                                                                                                                                              |
| -----: | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  **1** | **Ordinary**          | An average person with no noteworthy development. Farmer, clerk, laborer, ordinary civilian, inexperienced guard.                                                    |
|  **2** | **Practiced**         | Has meaningful training, education, occupational experience, or physical conditioning. Above an average nobody, but still completely ordinary by societal standards. |
|  **3** | **Skilled**           | A genuinely competent civilian or low-level professional. Good tradesman, trained hunter, competent guard, experienced sailor, trained fighter.                      |
|  **4** | **Trained**           | Serious professional training. Career soldier, established guard, martial artist, competent adventurer, trained specialist.                                          |
|  **5** | **Professional**      | Clearly stands above ordinary trained people. Veteran guard, capable officer, strong mercenary, accomplished specialist.                                             |
|  **6** | **Expert**            | One of the stronger members of a normal profession or organization. Considerably more capable than average professionals.                                            |
|  **7** | **Veteran**           | Extensive practical experience combined with significant ability. A dangerous and reliable individual even among trained people.                                     |
|  **8** | **Distinguished**     | Stands out among veterans. Often a specialist, decorated combatant, strong officer, respected adventurer, or equivalent.                                             |
|  **9** | **Veteran Elite**     | Near the ceiling of what an ordinary professional organization routinely produces. Difficult for normal trained people to challenge individually.                    |
| **10** | **Elite**             | One of the best members of an ordinary organization. The beginning of genuinely notable characters.                                                                  |
| **11** | **High Elite**        | Exceptional even among elites. Usually belongs to special units, prestigious organizations, major guilds, etc.                                                       |
| **12** | **Regional Elite**    | Reputation and ability can extend across a region. A person whose name is likely known within their profession.                                                      |
| **13** | **Exceptional**       | Clearly separated from conventional elites. Ordinary organizations may have nobody at this level.                                                                    |
| **14** | **Renowned**          | A widely recognized exceptional individual. Their accomplishments have begun establishing a serious reputation.                                                      |
| **15** | **Extraordinary**     | Far beyond ordinary professionals. Large numbers of merely trained opponents cease to be meaningful peers.                                                           |
| **16** | **Master**            | Has reached true mastery. Even other highly trained individuals recognize their superiority.                                                                         |
| **17** | **Grandmaster**       | Among the greatest practitioners of their field. Rare even across entire nations.                                                                                    |
| **18** | **Legendary**         | One of the exceptional mortals of an era. Their accomplishments can become historical or legendary.                                                                  |
| **19** | **Near-Peak Human**   | Has approached the true limits of humanity. Only the greatest mortals can progress further.                                                                          |
| **20** | **Peak Human**        | The recognized maximum of conventional human capability.                                                                                                             |
| **21** | **Limit-Breaker**     | Has surpassed the conventional human limit while still remaining fundamentally mortal.                                                                               |
| **22** | **Superhuman**        | The absolute mortal ceiling. Genuinely superhuman capability, but still within the furthest possible reach of a mortal.                                              |
| **23** | **Inhuman**           | Beyond what mortality alone can produce. First truly non-mortal level of development.                                                                                |
| **24** | **Ascendant**         | Significantly beyond the basic Inhuman threshold; clearly dominates most merely superhuman/in-human entities.                                                        |
| **25** | **Transcendent**      | Peak of the Strong tier. Has transcended the normal supernatural elite and stands immediately below Apex.                                                            |
| **26** | **Apex**              | Entry into the tiny uppermost class of beings.                                                                                                                       |
| **27** | **High Apex**         | Exceptional even among Apex beings.                                                                                                                                  |
| **28** | **Supreme Apex**      | Absolute Apex ceiling.                                                                                                                                               |
| **29** | **Pinnacle**          | Beyond Apex. Extremely rare.                                                                                                                                         |
| **30** | **Absolute Pinnacle** | Maximum level and XP progression ceiling. <br>**World Tree / World Serpent tier.**                                                                                   |

## The major thresholds

This gives us a very clean set of boundaries:

> **1 — Ordinary person**  
> **4 — Serious training begins**  
> **7 — Veteran**  
> **10 — Elite**  
> **13 — Exceptional**  
> **16 — Master**  
> **18 — Legendary**  
> **20 — Peak Human**  
> **21 — Limit-Breaker**  
> **22 — Superhuman / Mortal Ceiling**  
> **23 — Inhuman**  
> **26 — Apex**  
> **29 — Pinnacle**  
> **30 — Absolute Pinnacle**
