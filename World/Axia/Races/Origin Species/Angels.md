Angels's ancestors were [[Crown Hominid]] populations that climbed into the canopy of the northern Tree and stayed there, on branches large enough that entire forests grew on top of them. They survived powerful winds, immense vertical drops, airborne predators, drifting sky islands, luminous plants, and constant exposure to pollen and sap saturated with natural energy.

The first adaptation was gliding: membranes stretched between arms, torso, and legs, allowing controlled descent between branches. Over time those membranes separated from the limbs entirely and became independent wing structures, and unlike any ordinary animal wing, an Angel's wings are threaded with extensive networks of Aura-conducting tissue.

Angels were also later called the Goddess Clan in some records.
## Grace

The canopy held more natural energy than a biological body can safely absorb. Early canopy creatures died constantly from uncontrolled growth, mutation, and Aura saturation. The ancestors of the Angels survived by learning to draw that energy into their wings, filter it, and release it in a stable form.

That is **Grace**: the Angel racial ability to absorb diffuse natural energy and convert it into ordered, biologically compatible Aura. The wings do the intake. A ring-shaped organ around the upper spine and skull regulates the circulation, and when it is working hard it produces the visible field later called a **halo**.

A halo is not a floating ornament. It is the Aura field of the regulatory organ made visible, appearing as a ring, an arc, a crown, or a cluster of luminous symbols behind or above the head. Every healthy Angel can manifest one, though at rest it is usually faint enough to miss.

Grace lets Angels sustain flight, heal their own bodies, accelerate plant growth, stabilise injured organisms, purify some kinds of harmful natural energy, and build temporary structures out of condensed Aura. All of this is racial and instinctive, and all of it stays modest without training. Even an untrained Angel can close a wound, save a dying plant, or throw up a brief shield of light.

## Manifestation

An Angel's body is partly held together by its own Aura. The flesh is entirely biological, but a portion of the apparent mass is sustained by Grace rather than tissue, and that gives the species a second inherited ability, **Manifestation**.

Through Manifestation an Angel can compress or expand its body by changing how much ordered Aura is supporting it. A smaller form costs less energy, conceals more easily, and moves efficiently through dense growth. A larger form can hold and direct vastly more Aura, at an enormous and continuous energy cost.

This is not shapeshifting. Angels keep the same fundamental anatomy and proportions at every scale; they cannot become animals or objects. What varies is size. One Angel may spend its whole life at roughly human scale, another may habitually hold a small fairy-like form, and an ancient Angel with access to immense natural energy may expand into a Giant-scale body.

## Body

Angels have the standard humanoid plan: two arms, two legs, one head. Resting height usually runs from about 1.5-2 meters, with Manifestation allowing substantial temporary or long-term variation on either side of that.

Every Angel has one pair of wings growing from the upper back, combining feather-like structures with thin, leaflike membranes. They are living organs, not Aura constructs. Young or weak Angels have a single pair; extremely old individuals may grow branching secondary structures that read as additional pairs, but these all extend from the same original wing system.

Angel eyes have luminous irises, and the pupils become hard to make out while Grace is active. Their ears are slightly elongated. Their faces tend to be symmetrical because Grace corrects a great many developmental irregularities during growth, which is not the same thing as being beautiful, and Angels are not universally beautiful by human standards.

Their skin covers the full range of human-like pigmentation, with metallic, luminous, or faintly iridescent undertones on top of it. Hair frequently carries unusual colours from Aura-reactive pigments: white, gold, silver, green, blue, black, red, violet. None of this indicates rank or morality.

The halo appears whenever the Angel absorbs or releases significant natural energy, and its pattern is unique to the individual, like a fingerprint. Some are simple rings. Others are branching crowns, eyes, geometric diagrams, or sets of overlapping circles.

## At the end of the Mythological Era

Angels hold the canopy of the northern World Tree, the nearby sky islands, and the highest polar mountains. Their earliest societies are organized around groves, flight routes, seasonal gatherings, and the distribution of natural energy.
