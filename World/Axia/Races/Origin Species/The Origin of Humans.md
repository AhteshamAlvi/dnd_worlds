Humans do not exist during the Mythological Era. At its close, the three [[0 Origin Species|origin species]] are the only widespread sapient humanoid peoples in the world. [[Humans|Humanity]] emerges gradually across the early Ancient Era, out of interbreeding between [[Ancient Giants]], [[Devils]], and [[Angels]].

It works because all three descend from the [[Crown Hominid]] and remain reproductively compatible. It takes as long as it does because most of the early results do not survive.

## Why the early hybrids fail

Early hybrid children vary enormously. Some inherit a functioning Auraframe. Some get wings, or partial Grace. Some develop horns, an Infernal Core, or sensitivity to Imposition. Many inherit weakened fragments of several systems at once.

Most of them are biologically unstable, and the reason is simple: **the three racial systems compete for the same Aura.**

- The Giant **Auraframe** tries to distribute Aura outward through mineralised flesh and bone.
- The Devil **Infernal Core** tries to compress Aura into a centralized reserve under rigid internal control.
- The Angel **wing and halo system** tries to draw Aura outward and cycle it between the body and the environment.

A hybrid that strongly inherits all three lives in permanent internal conflict — Aura pulled into the skeleton, compressed toward the Core, and projected out through the wings simultaneously. Those individuals commonly suffer developmental abnormalities, shortened lives, sterility, or Aura fluctuations they cannot control.

The viable results fall into three categories:

1. **One complete system.** These are the most viable early hybrids. They get absorbed back into the corresponding parent species, or become the ancestors of later specialised peoples.
2. **Partial versions of two systems.** These lineages eventually produce a great many of the other humanoid species in the world — a story that belongs to later eras rather than this one.
3. **None of the three.** These become Humans.

## What the third category loses

Children in the third category have no Auraframe. No Infernal Core, horns, wings, halo, Grace, Imposition, Manifestation, or Lithic Resonance. Their bodies are smaller, physically weaker, shorter-lived, and by any contemporary standard less impressive than those of any origin species.

What they also lose is the enormous biological cost of maintaining those systems, and that turns out to be a massive advantage.

An Ancient Giant pours tremendous nutrition and Aura into every child; pregnancies are long and children take decades to mature. A Devil must form an Infernal Core during development, which makes reproduction slow and prone to failure. An Angel must grow a wing system, a halo organ, and an Aura-stabilised body, requiring concentrated natural energy throughout childhood.

Descendants carrying none of the three can be born on a fraction of that. They mature fast, eat comparatively little, survive in places where natural energy is thin, and reproduce far more often than any origin species. Their pregnancies are shorter, their children independent sooner, and a single population can multiply several times over within one Angel, Devil, or Giant generation.

Humanity is therefore not a weakened branch of one origin species. **It is the stable biological convergence of all three.**

The Human body keeps the shared Crown Hominid anatomy and discards the extreme specializations built on top of it. No universal horns, wings, halos, mineralized frames, Aura cores, or racial supernatural abilities. Human appearance is correspondingly combined — the general frame and resilience (as well as high physical ceiling) of Giant ancestors, the irises of Devils (resulting in many colors of eyes), the unusual pigmentation of Angels (resulting in many colors of hair), in every possible combination. None of those features necessarily carries the racial ability it came from.

## The three Human advantages

**Aura flexibility.** Ancient Giant Aura is drawn naturally through the Auraframe. Devil Aura compresses, imposes structure. Angel Aura circulates outward and engages living systems and ambient natural energy. Those tendencies grant immediate power at the price of a strong inherited bias.

Human Aura has no predetermined architecture at all. It can be distributed, compressed, projected, shaped, concealed, transformed, or restricted without fighting a racial system for the privilege. Humans start with less Aura in a far weaker body, and can develop a wider range of personal abilities than anyone with the 6 types of Aura. Their [[Hatsu]] are not obliged to complement a Auraframe, an Infernal Core, or a Grace system. This is exactly why Humans produce such strange, individual, hard-to-counter [[0 Nen|Nen]] abilities: they can imitate techniques from all three origin species and combine ideas the origin peoples consider naturally incompatible.

**Biological adaptability.** Requiring little natural energy and carrying few specialized organs, Humans survive across a far wider range of climates, diets, altitudes, and Aura densities. Plains, deserts, coasts, forests, mountains, and islands that would be inefficient or actively hostile to an origin species are ordinary Human territory.

**Speed of change.** Human generations are short enough that populations adapt within centuries where Giants, Devils, and Angels need millennia. Traits that help survival spread on a timescale the elder peoples cannot match, and neither can their institutions.

## There is no first Human

Humanity does not begin with a single union or a founding couple. Across many mixed settlements, more and more children are born without any of the three ancestral racial systems. Those children interbreed with each other and produce offspring consistently like themselves. Once two unmarked parents reliably produce unmarked children, the species exists. The Human phenotype itself is simply a convergent evolutionary position of losing all the original racial traits.

The origin species initially misread what has happened. Giants see undersized, malformed descendants. Devils call them **hollow**, for lacking both an Infernal Core and horns. Angels see diminished things incapable of sustaining Grace. For several generations Humans are treated as failed hybrids rather than a new people.

The advantage only shows up with time. A Giant settlement produces a handful of children in a century. A Devil house carefully raises one new generation. An Angel grove is essentially unchanged after hundreds of years. A Human village becomes a city inside the lifespan of a single elder from the origin peoples.

Humanity spreads not by out-fighting the three origin species individually — it cannot — but because Humans can live almost anywhere, reproduce quickly, learn from every culture they touch, and reorganize their societies faster than the elder peoples can respond to them.

That is the exchange that defines the species. Humans lose the inherited power of the Ancient Giants, the structured Aura of the Devils, and the living Grace of the Angels. In return they get fertility, adaptability, variation, and freedom.
