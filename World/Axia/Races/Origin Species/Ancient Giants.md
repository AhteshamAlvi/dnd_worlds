Ancient Giant's ancestors were [[Crown Hominid]] populations living around the base of the northern trunk, up on the polar mountains, and across the immense root-ridges that broke through the surface. They fed on the enormous plants, fungi, animals, and mineral-rich root growths the Tree produced, and they were hunted the entire time by megafauna larger, faster, and better armored than they were.

Their first answer to that was simply to get bigger. Size let them reach higher vegetation, push through deep snow, hold off predators, and retain and produce heat, and the natural energy saturating their food let the increase run far past what ordinary biology permits. Size alone, though, is a trap: a body scaled that far up collapses under its own weight.

## The Auraframe

The adaptation that made Giants viable was a **mineralized Aura lattice** running through the skeleton, muscle, and connective tissue. Trace metals and crystalline compounds taken in through the Tree's root growth were incorporated into their bones, and Aura began circulating through those structures automatically, reinforcing the body from inside. Their skeleton does not hold their mass up physically so much as carry a continuous field of reinforcement through the whole creature.

That inherited structure is the **Auraframe**, and it is the central racial ability of every Ancient Giant.

The Auraframe is permanent, unconscious bodily reinforcement. It is not [[Ten]], [[Ken]], [[Shū]], or any other formal [[0 Nen|Nen]] technique, though a trained Ancient Giant can layer all of those on top of it. Even an Ancient Giant who has never heard of Nen has Aura distributing itself through its body as a matter of course, keeping its bones from snapping under its own weight and enormously raising its strength, durability, and resistance to pressure.

The consequence is that an Ancient Giant does not experience strength the way a human does. Body, Aura, and mass reinforce one another in a loop: as the Ancient Giant grows larger and accumulates more Aura, the Auraframe strengthens, which permits the body to grow larger still. Size, age, and power are therefore braided together for this species in a way they are for no other. An old Ancient Giant is not merely experienced. It is bigger.

## Lithic Resonance

Ancient Giants carry a second racial sense, **Lithic Resonance**. Through direct contact with solid matter, they can feel the structure and shape, even gleaning secrets. An Ancient Giant standing barefoot on the ground can pick up distant movement, unstable rock, hollows underneath, and how weight is distributed through a structure. It is not precise enough to substitute for [[En]], but it gives the species an instinctive read on terrain and material that nothing else has.

## Body

Ancient Giants have a consistent humanoid body plan: two arms, two legs, one head, upright posture. Adults usually stand between twenty and forty meters, and extremely old individuals can go considerably beyond that. They are broad and dense, with thick torsos, heavy limbs, large hands, powerful necks. Even a lean Ancient Giant looks more solidly built than any other people because of the density of its bone and connective tissue.

Their skin is coarse and runs to earthen colors: brown, grey, bronze, dark red, clay, black, muted green. Their nails are thick and mineral-like. Their teeth carry pronounced canines inherited from an omnivorous ancestry.

Every Ancient Giant has **two symmetrical horn-ridges** on the upper sides of the skull. These are not ornaments and have nothing to do with [[Devils|Devil]] ancestry. They are extensions of the Auraframe, and they route Aura and physical shock away from the head. They generally sweep backward along the skull rather than projecting forward, and their shape varies between individuals, but no natural Ancient Giant lacks them.

## At the end of the Mythological Era

Ancient Giants are the most physically powerful of the three origin peoples. They held the ground around the northern trunk, and they built the first large permanent structures anywhere in the world: stone circles, windbreaks, burial cairns, carved migration markers, and vast shelters cut directly into mountainsides.
