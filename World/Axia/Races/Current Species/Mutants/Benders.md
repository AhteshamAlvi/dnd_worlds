Benders are [[0 Mutants|Mutants]] born with the ability to control a single natural element. They can emerge among any Human population, regardless of ancestry, geography, or whether anyone else in the family has ever possessed the same ability.

Bending originates from dormant [[Angel]] genes carried by Humanity.

[[Human|Humans]] descended from the gradual mixture and sublimation of [[Angel|Angels]], [[Devil|Devils]], and [[Ancient Giant|Ancient Giants]]. Most of the specialised racial structures belonging to those three species were lost during Humanity’s formation, but fragments of their developmental instructions remain buried throughout the Human body.

In a Bender, a portion of the Angelic system responsible for [[Angels#Grace|Grace]] reawakens during development.

The Human body cannot reconstruct true Grace. It cannot grow functional Angel wings, form a complete halo organ, or absorb and order every form of natural energy. Instead, the incomplete system stabilises by binding itself to one narrow pattern of natural energy.

That pattern becomes the Bender’s element.

## Elemental Circuit

Every Bender possesses an abnormal network of Aura-conducting tissue called an **Elemental Circuit**.

The Circuit develops primarily around the lungs, heart, spine, nervous system, and major joints. Thin branches extend through the arms, hands, legs, feet, eyes, and inner ear, allowing the Bender to sense and influence their element through both movement and perception.

The Elemental Circuit is an incomplete descendant of the biological system that allows Angels to use Grace. Angel wings absorb diffuse natural energy and convert it into ordered, biologically compatible Aura. A Bender’s Circuit performs a narrower version of the same process.

Rather than filtering natural energy generally, the Circuit resonates with one elemental pattern. It recognises that element in the environment, draws compatible natural energy from it, and uses the Bender’s Aura to direct its movement and behaviour.

This ability is called **Bending**.

Bending is not a [[Hatsu]]. It is an inherited biological function produced by the Mutation. A Bender can use it instinctively without understanding formal Nen, although their control remains crude until they learn how to regulate their Aura properly.

A trained Bender can still develop an entirely separate Hatsu. That Hatsu may complement their element, expand what they can do with it, or have nothing to do with Bending at all.

## Bending

A Bender can sense, attract, repel, redirect, compress, accelerate, and reshape their element by extending Aura through the Elemental Circuit.

The element is not moved by invisible physical force alone. The Bender’s Aura briefly establishes a sympathetic connection between their body and compatible matter or energy. Movement, breathing, posture, and intention determine how that connection is directed.

This is why Bending is commonly practised through martial forms.

The movements are not arbitrary traditions. They help the Elemental Circuit organise Aura through the correct parts of the body. A sweeping arm motion may guide a large volume of water, a rooted stance may connect an Earthbender to the ground, and a sharp breath may compress the Aura needed to ignite flame.

Skilled Benders can eventually reduce or eliminate obvious movements, but doing so requires much greater precision and concentration.

The amount an individual can control depends upon their Aura reserves, physical condition, distance from the element, familiarity with the material, and the resistance being exerted against them.

Matter saturated with another person’s Aura is much harder to Bend. A stone reinforced with [[Shū]], fire created and controlled by another Bender, or water enclosed within a living body resists outside control because another Aura pattern already claims it.

For the same reason, Benders cannot casually manipulate the internal elements of living creatures. Bloodbending, forcing air from someone’s lungs, disrupting the minerals in their bones, or igniting tissue from within requires overwhelming the target’s Aura and bypassing the natural boundary of their body. Such techniques are extremely rare, difficult, and usually impossible against a prepared Nen user of comparable strength.

## Elemental Resonance

Every Elemental Circuit stabilises around one exact **Elemental Resonance**.

A Bender cannot choose their element, change it through training, or expand into a related element. The resonance is fixed during development by the precise structure of the Mutation and remains the only elemental pattern that the Circuit can recognise and control.

A Firebender can bend fire, heat and nothing else. They cannot learn to bend lightning, or lava merely because those phenomena are associated with fire.

A Lightningbender can bend lightning and electrical charge, but cannot create or control ordinary flame.

An Earthbender can bend earth, stone, sand, crystal, and soil, but cannot bend metal, or lava unless their specific Mutation is aligned with one of those materials instead.

Every form of Bending is therefore its own distinct Mutation.

The four most common Elemental Resonances are:
- **Fire**
- **Water**
- **Earth**
- **Air**

Even these four are rare among the general population. Most communities may go generations without producing a Bender, and a large city may contain only a small number of them.

Other Elemental Resonances are vastly rarer. Metal, lightning, lava, sound, plants, and similar phenomena are not advanced applications of the four common elements. Each requires its own independently formed Elemental Circuit.

The resemblance between certain elements reflects their physical relationship, not shared Bending ability.

An Earthbender and a Metalbender may use similar movements because both manipulate dense solid matter, but their Circuits are fundamentally incompatible. An Earthbender’s Aura cannot establish resonance with metal, while a Metalbender may be completely incapable of moving an ordinary stone.

Likewise, a Firebender may survive near intense heat more easily than most people, but they cannot direct electricity. A Lightningbender may generate enormous electrical discharges while being no more capable of controlling a campfire than an ordinary Human.

No Bender naturally possesses more than one Elemental Resonance. A person born capable of bending two distinct elements would be undergoing multiple simultaneous Mutations and would be extraordinarily rare even among Benders.

### Firebenders

A Firebender’s Elemental Circuit resonates specifically with flame and combustion.

Their Circuit converts Aura and natural energy into ignition, allowing them to generate flame by heating and igniting surrounding gases. They can also control existing fire, change its shape and direction, increase or reduce its intensity, or extinguish it by disrupting the combustion reaction.

Firebending is closely tied to breath and internal rhythm. The lungs regulate intake, the torso compresses Aura, and the limbs provide direction. Weak breathing, physical exhaustion, and disrupted concentration greatly reduce a Firebender’s output.

Firebenders possess increased resistance to heat and ordinary flame, particularly their own, but they are not immune to all thermal damage. Fire strengthened by another person’s Aura, unusually intense heat, or prolonged exposure can still injure them.

Firebending grants no control over lightning, magma, smoke, or heat without flame. Those are separate Elemental Resonances.

### Waterbenders

A Waterbender’s Elemental Circuit resonates specifically with liquid water.

They can move, reshape, compress, accelerate, and redirect water, but their influence weakens when the substance ceases to remain liquid water. They can freeze water, manipulate ice, control steam, and command mist.

A Waterbender may alter temperature using an ordinary Nen technique, environmental tools, or a separate Hatsu, but such actions are not inherent parts of Waterbending.

Waterbenders cannot create large quantities of water from nothing. They require an existing source, though skilled users can gather small amounts from damp soil, plants, air, clothing, or stored containers.

Because their Circuit descends from the stabilising mechanisms of Angelic Grace, some Waterbenders become exceptionally skilled healers. Water serves as a medium through which they circulate Aura into injured tissue, allowing them to clean wounds, reduce inflammation, stabilise organs, and support recovery.

This healing is not guaranteed in every Waterbender and does not grant control over blood. Blood is living tissue saturated with the owner’s Aura and is not treated by the Circuit as ordinary liquid water.

### Earthbenders

An Earthbender’s Elemental Circuit resonates with natural earth, soil, sand, crystal, and stone.

They can sense and manipulate these materials, particularly when maintaining direct contact with the ground. Their control is strongest over unrefined stone and compacted soil and becomes weaker against heavily processed or Aura-saturated material.

Earthbenders possess an instinctive awareness of vibrations and structural pressure travelling through the ground. This can allow them to detect movement, hollow spaces, unstable terrain, and incoming impacts.

Earthbending does not include metal, or lava. Although these substances may originate from geological material, their structures are distinct enough that an Earth Circuit cannot properly resonate with them.

### Airbenders

An Airbender’s Elemental Circuit resonates with ordinary atmospheric air.

They can generate currents, compress or disperse air, redirect wind, manipulate pressure, and create bursts of force. Because breathable atmosphere surrounds them in most environments, Airbenders rarely lack access to their element.

Their Circuit is deeply connected to the lungs, inner ear, skin, and balance system. This grants heightened sensitivity to pressure changes, moving bodies, incoming projectiles, and disturbances in nearby airflow.

Airbenders can use controlled currents to soften falls, accelerate movement, redirect attacks, leap enormous distances, and produce cutting or concussive pressure.

They cannot automatically bend sound. Sound travels through air, but its defining pattern is vibration rather than atmosphere itself. 

## Exceptional Resonances

All Elemental Resonances outside fire, water, earth, and air are considered **Exceptional Resonances**.

They are not necessarily more powerful than the four common forms. They are more unusual because the Elemental Circuit must stabilise around a narrower, less abundant, or more complex natural pattern.

A Fire Circuit can stabilise around a phenomenon encountered almost everywhere: combustion. An Earth Circuit resonates with one of the most abundant forms of matter in the world. By contrast, a Lightning Circuit must regulate rapidly moving electrical charge without destroying its host, while a Metal Circuit must identify and manipulate precise metallic structures rather than broad natural stone.

This makes Exceptional Benders extraordinarily uncommon.

### Lightningbenders

A Lightningbender’s Elemental Circuit resonates with electrical charge and current.

They can generate differences in charge, guide electrical discharge, redirect existing electricity, and release concentrated bolts through their bodies. Their Circuit reinforces the heart, nerves, and muscles against the destructive effects of their own element.

Lightningbenders cannot bend fire. Their ability produces light and heat as side effects, but the Circuit interacts only with electrical phenomena.

Control is difficult because lightning moves extremely quickly and naturally follows conductive paths. A minor mistake can cause the current to pass through the Bender’s own organs or strike unintended targets.

### Metalbenders

A Metalbender’s Elemental Circuit resonates specifically with metallic matter.

They can sense the internal lattice, density, tension, and impurities within metal, allowing them to bend, compress, sharpen, twist, or reshape it.

Metalbenders do not require impurities or fragments of earth inside the metal. They control the metal itself. Refined metal may actually be easier for some Metalbenders to manipulate because its structure is more regular, while unusual alloys may be difficult until the Bender learns their composition.

They cannot bend stone or soil. An object containing both metal and stone may require the Metalbender to manipulate only the metallic portions.

Aura-forged alloys and metal reinforced through [[Shū]] can resist control, especially when another Nen user is actively maintaining ownership over the material.

### Lavabenders

A Lavabender’s Circuit resonates with molten rock.

They can move, shape, accelerate, and redirect magma or lava while resisting much of its heat. They cannot bend solid stone or ordinary flame.

Creating lava requires melting existing rock, which demands enormous heat and Aura unless a natural source is already present. Most Lavabenders are therefore far more dangerous near volcanoes, deep fissures, or other regions where molten material is accessible.

### Soundbenders

A Soundbender’s Circuit resonates with mechanical vibration.

They can amplify, suppress, redirect, focus, and distort sound waves travelling through matter. They may throw voices across great distances, create zones of silence, generate concussive bursts, or use reflected vibrations to sense their surroundings.

Soundbending does not grant control over air. Air is merely one medium through which sound may travel. A Soundbender can also manipulate vibrations passing through water, stone, metal, or other substances.

### Plantbenders

A Plantbender’s Circuit resonates with living plant tissue.

They can encourage growth, direct movement, alter the arrangement of roots and branches, and sense the condition of nearby plants. Their Mutation is one of the closest surviving echoes of Angelic Grace.

Plantbenders cannot create plants from nothing and cannot control dead wood as easily as living vegetation. The Aura of another living creature also resists interference, making the manipulation of intelligent or Aura-rich plant life significantly more difficult.

## Rarity

All Benders are uncommon.

The four broadly stable Resonances—fire, water, earth, and air—appear often enough that many cultures recognise them and have developed dedicated training traditions. Even so, they remain rare Mutations rather than normal members of the population.

Exceptional Resonances may appear only a handful of times within an entire historical period.

A kingdom might maintain schools for Firebenders and Earthbenders while knowing of only one Lightningbender in living memory. Some Exceptional Resonances are so rare that each known bearer is initially mistaken for a unique Mutant rather than a recurring form of Bending.

The rarity of a resonance does not automatically determine its strength. A Lightningbender may possess less Aura and combat experience than a skilled Firebender. What makes the Lightningbender exceptional is the unlikely structure of their Elemental Circuit, not guaranteed superiority.

## Inheritance

Bending can be inherited, but the exact element is not reliably preserved.

The child of a Firebender might inherit fire, develop a different resonance, carry a dormant and nonfunctional Circuit, or possess no Bending Mutation at all. Two non-Benders may produce a Bender when dormant Angelic traits combine into a functional Circuit.

Exceptional Resonances are particularly unstable. A Lightningbender’s children are more likely to possess no Bending at all than to inherit lightning. Even when several members of one family are Benders, their elements may differ.

A family that repeatedly produces Benders may eventually become a [[Clan]], especially if it preserves specialised training methods. However, the existence of a Firebending Clan does not prevent unrelated Firebenders from appearing elsewhere.

The Clan passes down a heightened likelihood of forming an Elemental Circuit, not exclusive ownership of the element.
