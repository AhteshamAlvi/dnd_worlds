## Humans

Humans are the baseline this whole world is measured against — the most numerous people by far, found in every kingdom, culture, and corner of it, and the species every other race here gets compared to when someone says "stronger than a human" or "passes for human." On their own, humans have no inherited racial gift: no claws, no elemental affinity, no built-in edge over anyone else. What they have instead is sheer range — the same species produces farmers, scholars, kings, and history's strongest Nen users, purely through will, training, and circumstance rather than anything handed to them at birth.

That blank slate is the point. Because humans start with nothing extra, whatever they become is built rather than born, and it's exactly why [[0 Nen|Nen]] took root the way it did — a system anyone can learn, with no race gatekeeping who gets to be powerful. The result is that humans hold their own against races built from monsters, gods, and magic bloodlines not despite having no innate trait, but because they never needed one.

Not every human stays purely human, of course — bloodlines change, chance intervenes, and the rest of this page covers how.

## The three tiers inside Humanity

Everything that is not baseline human sorts into one of three tiers, and the question that sorts it is *who inherits it* — not how powerful it is, and not how many people have it. The full standard, including the thresholds, is in [[Classification]].

| Tier | The claim it makes | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| [[0 Mutants|Mutants]] | "This happened to me." | An individual occurrence — spontaneous, triggered, or survived. Possibly heritable; still individual. |
| [[0 Clans|Clans]] | "Our family carries this." | A socially recognized lineage preserving a trait, technique, office, or identity. Any size. |
| [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtypes]] | "Our population is born this way, and we are still Human." | A stable physiological package normal across many independent lineages. |

They form a ladder rather than three separate boxes. One person is altered — a [[0 Mutants|Mutant]]. If the alteration breeds true, their descendants become a [[0 Clans|Clan]], and the ancestor is a Mutant still. If the trait then spreads across enough independent lineages that no bearer needs to be anyone's descendant to carry it, it becomes a [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtype]]. And if the whole developmental baseline eventually diverges — not one trait but the entire template the body is built from — what comes out the far end is no longer Human at all, but a species of its own ([[0 Current Species|Current Species]]).

Most human variation comes from one of two roots: descent from a [[Bloodkin]], or descent from a hybrid between a human and a member of another race. A few groups are set apart by caste or circumstance rather than by the body; the tiers accommodate that, since a [[0 Clans|Clan]] can preserve a political identity as readily as a trait in the blood.

The step past the top of that ladder is a real one, not a formality. A single inherited ability never crosses it: a million people carrying nothing but the Sharingan would be a million [[Uchiha]] Clan members, not a race.
