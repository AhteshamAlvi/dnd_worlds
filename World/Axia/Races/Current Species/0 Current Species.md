Everything on this page is **alive now**. A race belongs here if it still exists in the modern world in numbers that can carry it forward — not a last survivor, not a preserved specimen, but a real population. The floor is low: a people down to its final ten or twenty, clinging on in one valley or one hidden colony, still counts, because it can still produce the next generation. A people that can no longer do that belongs in [[0 Secondary Species|Secondary Species]] instead.

Most of what follows descends, eventually, from the three [[0 Origin Species|Origin Species]] — the family tree runs [[Crown Hominid]] → [[Ancient Giants]], [[Devils]], [[Angels]] → the lineages here. [[Humans]] are the largest branch of that tree by an enormous margin and are documented separately.

Not everything here is a species in the strict sense. Some of these categories cannot breed at all and persist anyway, because the world keeps *making* more of them — a death produces a [[Hollow]], collective feeling produces a [[Spirits|Spirit]], an artisan produces a [[0 Artificial Species|Construct]]. They are on this page for the same reason a breeding people is: encounter one today and it is there.

None of these races are inherently tied to [[0 Nen|Nen]]. Every one of them can produce Nen users, same as a human.

## Peoples

Born, breeding, and cultured — each its own species with homelands, history, and society the way humans have theirs.

- [[Beastfolk]] — the animal-traited spectrum, from *Marked* through *Mink* to shifting lineages
- [[Dragon]]
- [[Dwarf]]
- [[Elf]] — near-human, pointed-eared, born with an affinity for Nen
- [[Fairy]] — inches tall, hidden, and pound-for-pound stronger than anything that size should be
- [[Giant]] — Elbaf's duelling culture and the earth-shaping mountain lines
- [[Humans]]  — The standard species; carries its own tree of [[0 Mutants|Mutants]], [[0 Clans|Clans]], and [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtypes]]
- [[Merfolk]] — fish-tailed peoples, *Fishmen*, and the [[Giant]]-descended *Imuchakk*
- [[Namekian]]
- [[Orckin]] — the tusked peoples and the smaller *Goblinkin*, both organised around stated obligation
- [[Plantfolk]] — grown from seed, centuries-lived, including the rooted *Treants*
- [[Saiyan]] — scattered and assimilated, no homeland left, still unmistakable
- [[Sky People]] — the upper-air peoples, including the rarefied **Angels** of the highest islands

## Beings that were never born

They do not reproduce. The world produces them, continuously, out of energy, death, or feeling.

- [[Elementals]] — natural energy alone, given shape and a will, with nothing human in it
- [[Hollow]] — a death that would not let go, fused with natural energy in the deep caverns; the ladder runs Hollow to *Arrancar*
- [[Spirits]] — condensed collective emotion, **Blessed** or **Cursed**

## Transformed

They were people first. What they are now is what happened to them.

- [[Vampires]] — *Originals* born to it, *Vampires* infected into it, *Made Originals* who outgrew the tether. Only the Originals are a species; a human turned by one has undergone a [[0 Mutants|Mutation]].

The [[Infernal]] entry moved to [[0 Mutants|Mutants]] — no one is ever born an Infernal, so the condition has no species tier to sit at.
