Aura and [[0 Nen|Nen]] are not synonymous. Aura is the sentient form of Life Force — the resource itself, described in [[Natural Energy, Life Force, and Aura]]. Nen is the discipline through which Aura is consciously perceived, controlled, released, suppressed, concentrated, distributed, and shaped. A person does not obtain Aura by awakening Nen; they already possess it by virtue of being sentient, and awakening only grants conscious access to what was already there. Aura defines the resource; Nen defines how that resource can be manipulated.

## Aura before awakening

The points on the body from which Aura flows are called **aura nodes** — additional pores in the body, though not on the skin; they sit on the spiritual body rather than the physical one. Aura is invisible to non-Nen users, and can only be seen once the aura nodes in the eyes are opened, though sensitive individuals can feel its presence without being consciously aware of what they're feeling.

In an unawakened, uncontrolled state, the Aura from all parts of the body tends to flow together into one mass, entirely without the individual's awareness. This normally results in Aura leaking slowly and constantly out of the body, streaming up and off the top of the head, without meaningfully harmful consequences. If someone's aura nodes are instead fully open and they make no attempt to close them or control the flow, they will grow exhausted far faster — eventually unable to stand, or even losing consciousness. Learning to control one's aura nodes, the discipline of [[Ten]], is the first step to becoming a Nen user, and it is precisely what stops that uncontrolled leak.

People who haven't trained in Ten leak Aura the way steam boils out of a kettle. It has been described as feeling like a warm, viscous fluid at rest to those sensitive enough to notice it; powerful, refined Aura instead produces a sensation closer to needles pricking the skin.

## Sensing Aura

Because every sentient being emits Aura, whether or not it has ever been consciously controlled, learning to sense Aura is a practical skill for tracking down living things or locating non-living objects that have been infused with it. An experienced Nen user can judge an opponent's location and rough strength just by reading the Aura they put out — and, in the other direction, a skilled user can regulate their own flow so carefully that they read as a beginner, or as an ordinary unawakened person, to anyone sensing them.
