Aura that has been brought into active use — drawn from the [[Aura Reserves|reserve]] via Output — can be distributed across a body, an object, or any other valid target. **Distribution** describes *where* that active Aura is located; **Density** describes *how concentrated* it is over the area it has been distributed across. Density is not an independent reserve statistic of its own — it is simply a consequence of how Aura has been distributed:

> Aura Reserves → Aura brought into use → Distribution → Density

Left alone, a creature's active Aura spreads evenly across its whole body: the standard whole body is treated as **100 Surface Units**, so an even distribution means the same density everywhere. More detailed regional distribution — concentrating Aura into one limb, one point, or one object rather than spreading it evenly — is a matter of trained control rather than the underlying Aura system itself. That control is what [[0 Nen Principles|the Nen principles]] provide: [[Gyō]] pulls Aura into a single region, [[Ken]] holds it evenly across the whole body at volume, [[Ryū]] redistributes it at any ratio in real time, and [[Kō]] pushes concentration to its extreme in one point at the cost of everywhere else. The Aura system itself only represents the quantity of Aura a creature has, where it currently sits, and the density that results — what a given creature is actually capable of *doing* with that placement is a question of Nen mastery, not of the reserve underneath it.
