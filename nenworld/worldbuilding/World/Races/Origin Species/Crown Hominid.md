The **Crown Hominid** is the common ancestor of every [[0 Origin Species|origin species]] and through them, of [[Humans|humanity]] itself. It is not a people and was never civilized. It was an animal, and it was already living throughout the World Tree's ecosystem long before the Tree changed anything.

It was a large omnivorous mammal with grasping hands, forward-facing eyes, and complex social behavior. It used simple objects, communicated by call and gesture, travelled in family groups, and made limited instinctive use of Aura. Its intelligence sat at roughly the ceiling of the cleverest [[Monsters|Magical Beasts]]: sharp, adaptable, capable of learning, but nowhere near sapient.

## The three isolations

Crown Hominid populations were separated by the geography of the Tree itself, which offered three habitable bands stacked vertically on top of one another:

- **The ground and lower trunk** — the polar mountains, the root-ridges breaking the surface, and the immense plant and animal life the Tree fed. These became the [[Ancient Giants]].
- **The subterranean root network** — hollow roots and cavern systems beneath the Tree, lightless and structurally unstable. These became the [[Devils]].
- **The upper canopy** — branches large enough to carry forests, plus the drifting sky islands around them. These became the [[Angels]].

Each band concentrated the Tree's power differently, and each population was shaped by the specific problem its band posed. None of the three diverged by choice or design. They diverged because they could not reach each other.

## Why the three remained compatible

The isolations lasted an immense span of time, but the three descendant species never stopped being close relatives. They retained the same fundamental humanoid body (two arms, two legs, one head, upright posture) along with compatible souls and compatible reproductive structures. They can produce fertile offspring with one another.
