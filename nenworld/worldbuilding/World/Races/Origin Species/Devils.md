Devils's ancestors were [[Crown Hominid]] populations that went down into the hollow roots and subterranean caverns beneath the northern Tree: absolute darkness, narrow passages, unstable Aura currents, toxic fungi, sudden floods, predatory burrowers, and pockets of natural energy dense enough to kill anything that walked into them unwarned.

The root network was not one environment but thousands. The Tree's Aura moved through channels, chambers, membranes, and barriers; some passages stayed open for centuries and then sealed, others opened only when pressure, temperature, or Aura density hit a particular value. Strength did not solve that. What solved it was the ability to recognize a boundary and understand the conditions under which it changes.

## Horns and thresholds

The subterranean population evolved sensory organs that read pressure, Aura density, living intention, and spatial discontinuity. Those organs became the **horns** of the Devil species. Devil horns are not primarily weapons, they are highly sensitive Aura organs wired directly into the nervous system.

Through them, Devils sense **spaces**.

A space is any meaningful division between one state and another: open and closed, inside and outside, etc. Early Devils read physical ones: sealed tunnels, unstable caverns, territorial marks, the edges of hostile Aura fields. As their intelligence developed, the same sense extended to social and psychological boundaries.

## Imposition

**Imposition** is the Devil racial ability, and it grew directly out of that sense.

Imposition lets a Devil give additional stability to a clearly expressed condition by investing Aura into it. The condition has to be understood and accepted by everyone it directly binds. A Devil cannot simply declare that another being must obey and have it become universally true. But when someone knowingly accepts a bargain, walks into a marked territory, takes a guarded object under stated terms, or voluntarily agrees to a restriction, the Devil's Aura can attach itself to that agreement and hold it there.

Simple use is instinctive. A Devil can mark an entrance, establish that passage is permitted only under a particular condition, and know immediately when an accepted bargain has been broken. Elaborate contracts, curses, seals, spatial restrictions, and rule-based abilities require formal [[0 Nen|Nen]] training.

Imposition is not reality manipulation. It is an inherited affinity for attaching Aura to consent, boundaries, and declared rules, and its strength depends on four things: the Aura invested, the clarity of the condition, the willingness of those bound by it, and the severity of the consequence. Every later Devil contract, commandment, barrier, seal, and magical law is built on it. This is also the origin of Nen contracts that become widespread and possible for every race. Devils simply have that contractual ability inherent to them, and are able to make or establish and enforce them inherently.

## The Infernal Core

Devils also carry a specialized organ, the **Infernal Core**: a compact secondary Aura reservoir sitting behind the sternum and partly wrapped around the heart. It exists because underground Aura fluctuations routinely wrecked ordinary biological function, and the individuals who could hold a stable internal reserve survived conditions that killed everything else in the tunnel.

The Core regulates the Devil's body and Aura independently of the surrounding environment. It grants exceptional resistance to hostile energy, disease, starvation, and swings in atmospheric natural energy, and it lets a Devil survive injuries that would kill another species outright. So long as the Core and the brain still function, the Devil can push stored Aura into restoring damaged tissue.

That regeneration is powerful but it is not unlimited. It burns enormous quantities of Aura, slows badly as the Core depletes, and cannot save the Devil if the Core itself is destroyed. A Devil whose Core has been shattered is as mortal as anything else.

## Body

Devils are humanoid and normally stand between 1.5-2 meters. They have long limbs, narrow waists, and unusually flexible joints. Two arms, two legs, one head, and no natural wings.

Every Devil has one pair of horns rising from the upper forehead or temples. They are symmetrical and packed with Aura-sensitive tissue, and damaging them meaningfully impairs the Devil's spatial perception. A Devil with broken horns is disoriented in a way that is hard to explain to anyone who has never had the sense.

They have pointed ears, dark sclera, and vividly coloured irises, with pupils that tend to be narrow or geometric rather than round. Skin ranges from ash-white and grey through dark brown, crimson, violet, blue-black, and nearly black. The variation is ordinary biological variation and carries no implication about morality or rank, whatever later cultures decided.

Lines of darker or lighter pigmentation follow the major Aura channels under the skin. Under significant exertion these become visible, and the Devil appears covered in glowing runes or branching patterns. Later civilisations read these as marks of sin, rank, house, or magical authority. They are birthmarks.

Devils have sharp canines and reinforced nails but are not obligate predators; their ancestors were opportunistic omnivores living on fungi, roots, insects, carrion, and whatever moved in the dark.

## At the end of the Mythological Era

Devils hold the cavern systems beneath the northern World Tree, and they form the first societies governed by **explicit conditions**. Their communities run on marked territory, recognised paths, rules for shared food, formal systems of challenge, and stated agreements between families.
