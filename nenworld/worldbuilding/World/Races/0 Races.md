## Contents

- [[0 Origin Species|Origin Species]] — the three peoples the world started with, and where everyone else came from
- [[0 Secondary Species|Secondary Species]] — their descendants that died out or became something else
- [[0 Current Species|Current Species]] — every race alive in the world today, [[Humans]] included

The three species folders are chronological: origin, the lineages in between, and where they arrived. Within [[0 Current Species|Current Species]], [[Humans]] carries its own tree — [[0 Mutants|Mutants]], [[0 Clans|Clans]], and [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtypes]] — because the great majority of documented human variation sits below the species line rather than at it.

## The two questions

Two different tests run across this whole folder, and it is worth not confusing them.

**What kind of thing is it?** Answered by [[Classification]]. A trait that landed on one person is a Mutation; a trait a family keeps is a Clan; a trait a whole population is born with is a Racial Subtype; a body plan that no longer needs its parent species to describe it is a Species. Population size settles exactly one of those boundaries — Clan to Racial Subtype — and even there only alongside lineage independence and biological stability.

**Does it still exist?** Answered by which species folder it sits in. A species that has declined to fifty survivors is still a species; it is simply a [[0 Secondary Species|vanished]] one. Numbers decide whether something is here now. They never decide what it is.
