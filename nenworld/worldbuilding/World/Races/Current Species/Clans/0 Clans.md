A **Clan** is any socially recognized lineage or extended kinship group that preserves something across generations. What it preserves does not matter to the classification: a [[0 Mutants|Mutation]] or inherited physical trait, a [[Nen Ability|Nen ability]] or unusual aura structure, a secret technique, a fighting style, a profession, a religious office, a political identity, or any mix of those. The [[Uchiha]] qualify on the Sharingan; the [[Yamanaka]] qualify on techniques and family identity alone, whether or not anything in their blood carries them.

**Size is not a disqualifier.** A Clan of hundreds of thousands is still a Clan so long as membership stays tied to a shared genealogy, house identity, adoption structure, or inherited institution. A family whose every child inherits its trait is a Clan with a dominant inheritance, not a population.

Where a Clan *does* stop being a Clan is when the trait outgrows it — carried by enough independent lineages that no bearer needs to be anyone's descendant to have it. That is a [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtype]], and the full test is in [[Classification]]. The individual at the far other end — the one the inheritance started with, before there was a family to carry it — is a [[0 Mutants|Mutant]].

The two groupings below are a worldbuilding convenience, not a second classification. Both are Clans.

## Bloodline clans

Clans whose inheritance is a real, bodily trait.

- [[Uchiha]] — Sharingan (the eyes physically change)
- [[Hyuga]] — Byakugan (eyes)
- [[Chinoike]] — Ketsuryūgan (eyes)
- [[Kurta]] — Scarlet Eyes; hunted to near-extinction for them
- [[Gojo]] — the Six Eyes; sees the structure of aura itself
- [[Kaguya]] — manipulate their own skeleton, bone as weapon
- [[Hozuki]] — bodies can turn to water
- [[Iburi]] — bodies turn to smoke, only partly under their control
- [[Kamo]] — their own blood moves at will
- [[Inumaki]] — born mouth-marked; their speech compels obedience
- [[Yuki]] — produce ice from the body; run cold, suffer in heat
- [[Jūgo Clan]] — absorb natural energy involuntarily; transform and rage
- [[Aburame]] — bodies host insect colonies from birth
- [[Inuzuka]] — feral physiology, animal-sharp senses
- [[Uzumaki]] — extreme vitality and longevity
- [[Ubuyashiki]] — hereditary illness and early death, paired with foresight
- [[Kurama Clan|Kurama Clan]] — illusions vivid enough to take physical effect
- [[Senju]]
- [[Vinsmoke]] — engineered lineage; [[0 Artificial Species|artificial]] work that bred true

The [[Fanalis]] were listed here until their trait was judged to have outgrown any one family; they are now filed under [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtypes]], with the reasoning and the open question recorded there.

## Other clans and houses

Families whose inheritance is technique, rank, wealth, or history rather than the body.

### Naruto
- [[Nara]] — shadow-manipulation techniques, taught only within the family
- [[Yamanaka]] — mind-transfer and mental intrusion techniques
- [[Akimichi]] — body-expansion techniques fuelled by stored calories
- [[Sarutobi]] — an old, distinguished line of leaders and teachers
- [[Hatake]] — a small, respected family of exceptional individual talent
- [[Shimura]] — political operators; the hand behind a lot of quiet decisions
- [[Kamizuru]] — insect-handling rivals to the [[Aburame]], long in decline
- [[Fūma]] — once an enormous shinobi clan, since splintered and scattered

### Jujutsu Kaisen
- [[Zenin]] — the third of the great sorcerer houses; brutally traditional, technique-obsessed

### Bleach
- [[Kuchiki]] — the most rigidly proper of the great noble houses
- [[Shihōin]] — a noble house tied to covert operations and stealth work
- [[Shiba]] — a once-great house fallen out of standing
- [[Ishida]] — the last family holding to the old [[Quincy]] traditions

### HunterXHunter
- [[Zoldyck]] — a dynasty of assassins who raise children into the trade from birth
- [[Nostrade]] — a criminal family trading in Nen users and rare goods

### One Piece
- [[Charlotte]] — an enormous dynasty built on marriage alliances and dozens of children
- [[Kozuki]] — the rightful ruling line of an isolated country
- [[Kurozumi]] — the usurping family that displaced them; a name treated as a curse
- [[Donquixote]] — descended from world nobility; some kept the title, some fled it
- [[Nefertari]] — a royal line that famously refused a seat among the world's rulers

### Black Clover
- [[Silva]] — a proud noble house of mercury magic
- [[Vermillion]] — a noble house of fire magic and ferocious tempers
- [[Kira]] — a noble house holding significant political rank
- [[Agrippa]] — a reclusive noble house with a grim reputation

### Demon Slayer

### Fairy Tail
- [[Heartfilia]] — a merchant dynasty of immense wealth

### My Hero Academia
- [[Todoroki]] — a family assembled deliberately to produce a stronger heir

### Magi: Kingdom of Magic

### Seven Deadly Sins

## Castes

Not one family, but a genealogy all the same — many houses holding a single inherited status between them. A caste passes down a political identity rather than a body, which the Clan tier explicitly covers.

- [[Celestial Dragons]] — dozens of separate noble houses sharing the founding generation, the legal untouchability, and nothing else; physically ordinary humans
