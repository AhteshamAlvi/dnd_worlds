Eternals originated during the early [[Ancient Era]], not long after [[Humans]] had stabilized as a distinct species. They were created by a civilization of [[Angels]].

The Angels regarded Humanity as an evolutionary contradiction. Humans had lost the specialized organs of [[Ancient Giants]], [[Devils]], and Angels, leaving them individually weaker than any of their ancestors. At the same time, that loss had made Human bodies extraordinarily adaptable, reproductively efficient, and flexible in their use of Aura.

The Celestials attempted to combine those advantages with the longevity and self-sustaining biology of Angels. They selected an early Human population and altered its development over several generations, placing a new Aura-processing structure throughout the body rather than restoring wings, halos, horns, an Infernal Core, or an Auraframe.

The resulting people were the Eternals.

Eternals are therefore not Angels in Human form, nor are they Humans who learned exceptionally powerful Nen. They are a stable engineered species descended from Humanity, designed to preserve the Human body while replacing its ordinary biological limitations with a permanent artificial Aura system.

## Celestial Matrix

Every Eternal possesses a **Celestial Matrix**, an artificial network of Aura-conducting tissue distributed throughout the entire body.

The Matrix begins around the nervous system and blood vessels, then branches through the muscles, bones, skin, and organs. No single part permanently functions as its core. Every section supports the others, turning the Eternal’s entire body into one continuous Aura-processing structure.

The Celestial Matrix was developed by the [[Celestial|Celestials]] from the biological principles behind [[Angel|Angelic]] [[Grace]]. Angel wings absorb natural energy from the environment, filter it, and convert it into ordered Aura. Eternals have no wings. Instead, the Matrix allows every part of their body to absorb minute quantities of diffuse natural energy.

That energy is filtered, stabilised, and incorporated into the Eternal’s Aura. The process is slow but continuous. An Eternal gradually replenishes its reserves even while sleeping or remaining completely inactive, provided that some natural energy exists in the surrounding environment.

Because the Matrix is spread throughout the body, an Eternal does not merely surround its flesh with Aura. Aura is physically woven into its biology. Muscles contract with supernatural force, bones withstand pressure that should pulverise them, and damaged organs continue operating under injuries that would immediately kill a Human.

This produces the universal physical qualities shared by all Eternals:
- Superhuman strength and durability
- Agelessness after physical maturity
- Extreme resistance to disease and poison
- Resistance to heat, cold, pressure, radiation, and hostile natural energy
- Rapid regeneration
- Continuous, gradual Aura recovery
- Compatibility with the Uni-Mind
- A far greater capacity for sustained Aura output than an ordinary Human

The passive reinforcement produced by the Matrix resembles a permanent low-level application of [[Chū]], but it is an artificial racial function rather than a consciously maintained Nen technique.

Damage to one part of the Matrix can be compensated for by the remainder. Completely killing an Eternal requires overwhelming most of the Matrix at once, exhausting its stored Aura, destroying the pattern that defines its body, or severing the connection between the Matrix and the Eternal’s soul.

All Celestial Matrices share this underlying structure, but they are not identical. The Celestials created several specialized **Matrix designs**, each built to process and express Aura in a different way.

An Eternal’s Matrix design determines its primary racial ability.

These abilities are not [[Hatsu]]. They are artificial racial functions embedded into the Eternal’s body before transformation. An Eternal may still develop an individual Hatsu through ordinary Nen training, but that Hatsu is separate from the ability provided by the Matrix.

## Matrix Designs

Celestial Matrices are divided into multiple "designs". Multiple Matrices can theoretically share the same design, although the number of surviving examples varies greatly. Each Matrix also adapts to its host, meaning two Eternals carrying the same design may differ in precision, scale, preferred application, and overall strength.

#### Arsenal Matrix

The **Arsenal Matrix** converts stored Aura into stable solid-energy constructs.

Its wielder can manifest weapons directly around the hands or body, including swords, spears, axes, bows, shields, chains, armour, and other comparatively simple implements. These objects possess no ordinary physical material. They are condensed structures of ordered Aura maintained by the Matrix.

An Arsenal construct remains stable only while connected to its creator’s Aura. It disappears when dismissed, shattered, or separated from the Eternal for too long.

The Matrix can reproduce any weapon structure the Eternal thoroughly understands. Complexity matters. A sword can be formed almost instinctively, while a complicated mechanical weapon requires detailed knowledge of every moving component.

The famous Eternal [[Thena]] carries an Arsenal Matrix.

#### Radiant Matrix

The **Radiant Matrix** specializes in propulsion and concentrated energy release.

It allows its wielder to generate sustained directional force around the body, providing true high-speed flight rather than simple levitation. The Eternal can accelerate, stop, hover, and change direction without wings or physical propulsion.

The same channels can release tightly concentrated beams of Aura through the eyes. The eyes serve as focusing organs rather than the actual source of the energy. These beams produce intense heat, force, and destructive pressure, with greater output requiring greater Aura expenditure.

The Radiant Matrix grants exceptional aerial mobility but does not make its wielder immune to the stresses of acceleration. The Eternal’s base Matrix must reinforce the body continuously during high-speed movement.

The famous Eternal [[Ikaris]] carries a Radiant Matrix.

#### Bulwark Matrix

The **Bulwark Matrix** concentrates Aura into an external reinforcement layer around selected parts of the body.

This layer appears as a luminous exoskeleton, most commonly around the hands, forearms, shoulders, and upper torso. It acts as both armour and a force-multiplying structure, allowing the Eternal to deliver impacts far beyond the limits of ordinary bodily reinforcement.

Unlike an Arsenal construct, the Bulwark is directly anchored to the Eternal’s body. It cannot normally be thrown, shaped into independent weapons, or maintained at a distance.

The more compactly the exoskeleton is concentrated, the stronger it becomes. A Bulwark Eternal may reinforce the entire body for general defence or compress nearly all available power into one arm for a single devastating strike.

The famous Eternal [[Gilgamesh]] carries a Bulwark Matrix.

#### Artillery Matrix

The **Artillery Matrix** compresses Aura into small, extremely dense projectiles.

Its wielder most commonly releases these projectiles through the fingertips. They can be fired rapidly as bullet-like bolts or accumulated into larger charged blasts with greater destructive power.

Unlike the Radiant Matrix, which produces continuous beams, the Artillery Matrix specializes in discrete bursts. Its projectiles remain cohesive after leaving the body and can travel considerable distances before dispersing.

The Eternal can adjust their size, velocity, detonation, and penetrative force. Rapid fire consumes relatively little Aura per shot, while a fully charged projectile can require a significant portion of the Eternal’s total reserve.

The famous Eternal [[Kingo]] carries an Artillery Matrix.

#### Velocity Matrix

The **Velocity Matrix** allows the body and mind to function at extreme speed.

It does not simply strengthen the legs. The Matrix accelerates neural processing, perception, muscle contraction, balance, Aura circulation, and the rate at which the body responds to environmental changes.

Without this complete acceleration, the Eternal would be unable to perceive obstacles, change direction, or withstand the forces generated by their own movement.

The Velocity Matrix continuously reinforces the body and the surface beneath the Eternal’s feet. At high speeds, the wielder can cross enormous distances, run across unstable surfaces, produce destructive sonic pressure, and strike with momentum comparable to much larger creatures.

The ability consumes immense Aura when sustained. Friction, terrain damage, rapid directional changes, and the protection of nearby people remain serious concerns even for a skilled user.

The famous Eternal [[Makkari]] carries a Velocity Matrix.

#### Transmutation Matrix

The **Transmutation Matrix** allows its wielder to alter the molecular structure of non-living matter through direct Aura contact.

The Eternal first reads the physical arrangement of the target, then imposes a different stable arrangement upon it. Stone can become wood, metal can become glass, and complex manufactured objects can be reduced to harmless substances.

The Matrix does not create matter from nothing. Mass must be retained or redistributed, and larger or more complex transformations require proportionally greater Aura and understanding.

Living matter resists the Matrix because a living creature’s Aura continually asserts its own biological form. The original Celestial design therefore restricted the Matrix to non-living matter. Overcoming that restriction would require an exceptional mutation, alteration of the Matrix, or an extraordinarily powerful Hatsu.

The famous Eternal [[Sersi]] carries a Transmutation Matrix.

#### Mirage Matrix

The **Mirage Matrix** projects structured sensory information directly into the surrounding environment and into the minds of observers.

Its illusions can reproduce light, sound, movement, apparent texture, spatial depth, and the visible presence of people or objects. Skilled users can disguise themselves, conceal entire groups, create false landscapes, or surround an enemy with a completely fabricated scene.

The Matrix does not ordinarily alter physical matter. An illusory wall cannot stop a real attack, although the victim may perceive themselves as having collided with it.

The more senses the illusion must deceive, the greater the Aura cost. Large-scale illusions are easier to maintain when most observers expect to see what is being presented.

The famous Eternal [[Sprite]] carries a Mirage Matrix.

#### Dominion Matrix

The **Dominion Matrix** specializes in establishing direct control over other minds.

It releases ordered Aura through telepathic contact and imposes a command structure upon the target’s nervous system. The Eternal may freeze a person in place, suppress particular actions, compel obedience, or coordinate the behaviour of large groups.

Dominion is not simple verbal persuasion. The Matrix directly interferes with the target’s ability to translate intention into action.

Strong Aura, disciplined [[Ten]], exceptional willpower, mental Nen abilities, or prior awareness can allow a target to resist. Controlling a large population also divides the Matrix’s attention and Aura across every affected individual.

A Dominion Eternal can control thousands of weak and unprepared minds more easily than one exceptionally powerful Nen user.

The famous Eternal [[Druig]] carries a Dominion Matrix.

#### Affect Matrix

The **Affect Matrix** stimulates, suppresses, and redirects emotion.

Its wielder can induce calm, pleasure, trust, courage, affection, infatuation, excitement, satisfaction, or emotional dependency. The Matrix acts upon the emotional and reward systems of the brain rather than issuing direct commands.

A target influenced by the Affect Matrix retains the technical ability to make choices, but those choices are made under artificially altered emotional conditions. A person overwhelmed with trust may willingly obey suggestions they would otherwise reject, while someone placed into absolute calm may cease resisting an immediate threat.

The Matrix can also suppress panic, reduce pain-related distress, settle violent crowds, and stabilise people suffering emotional shock. Its capacity for healing and manipulation are inseparable.

Strong Aura and emotional self-awareness provide some resistance, but the ability is especially dangerous because victims may believe their altered feelings are entirely their own.

The famous Eternal [[Eros]] carries an Affect Matrix.

#### Forge Matrix

The **Forge Matrix** converts ordered Aura into temporary mechanisms, tools, and technological constructs.

Unlike the Arsenal Matrix, which produces comparatively simple weapons, the Forge Matrix is capable of forming complex moving systems. Its wielder can create interfaces, restraints, engines, mechanical limbs, defensive fields, weapons, and intricate devices from condensed Aura.

The Matrix does not grant automatic knowledge. The Eternal must understand the principles behind whatever is being built. Its true racial gift is the ability to visualise, calculate, assemble, and maintain complex structures at extraordinary speed.

Permanent machinery still requires real material. Aura constructs eventually disappear when their energy supply is removed. A Forge Eternal therefore often uses temporary constructs to manufacture, repair, or manipulate permanent objects.

The famous Eternal [[Phastos]] carries a Forge Matrix.

#### Shepherd Matrix

The **Shepherd Matrix** was created to preserve the Eternal population, regulate the succession of Celestial Matrices, and maintain communication with the Angels.

It allows its wielder to extend the restorative pattern of the Celestial Matrix into another body. A Shepherd can stabilise injuries, purge harmful Aura, accelerate regeneration, repair damaged organs, and restore sections of another Eternal’s Matrix that have become fragmented or disconnected.

The Shepherd Matrix is particularly effective upon Eternals because it can directly read their. It does not need to determine what the body is supposed to look like; the patient’s own Matrix provides a complete pattern that the Shepherd can reinforce and repair.

A Shepherd can also heal [[Humans]] and other biological species, although this is more difficult. Without an Eternal Form to follow, the Shepherd must interpret the patient’s anatomy, Aura pathways, injuries, and natural biological pattern directly. Complex damage, unusual species, and unfamiliar racial organs therefore require far greater knowledge and precision.

More importantly, the Shepherd Matrix is the only Matrix design capable of manipulating another Celestial Matrix as an independent structure.

An ordinary Eternal can activate, strengthen, and regulate their own Matrix, but they cannot safely remove it from their body, implant it into another person, construct a new one, or guide it through the transformation of a Human host. Even a Forge Matrix user can only build the machinery and physical infrastructure required for these procedures. The actual separation, stabilization, soul-binding, and implantation of a Matrix can be performed only through a Shepherd Matrix.

The Shepherd Matrix contains several specialized functions unavailable to any other design.

It can read the complete internal pattern of a Celestial Matrix, distinguish the Matrix from the body and soul of its bearer, and separate the three without immediately destroying them. It can condense a distributed Matrix into a stable Matrix Seed, preserve that Seed outside a living body, and implant it into a compatible Human host.

A Shepherd can also oversee the growth of a new Matrix, assign it a recognised design template, stabilize its developing Eternal Form, and guide its first connection to a Human soul. Without a Shepherd, a newly grown Matrix remains an incomplete Aura-organ incapable of becoming a living Eternal.

While the Angels still lived, Shepherds served as intermediaries, healers, spiritual authorities, administrators, and custodians of Eternal succession. They decided when a damaged Matrix could be repaired, when a dying Eternal should surrender their Matrix, and which Humans were suitable to receive one.

After the Angels abandoned the Eternals and later died out, the Shepherds became the sole remaining means through which the Eternal population could continue.

This makes every Shepherd uniquely important. The death of an Arsenal, Radiant, or Dominion bearer may destroy one Matrix and reduce the Eternal population by one. The destruction of a Shepherd Matrix eliminates part of the species’ ability to preserve every other Matrix.

Should the final Shepherd Matrix ever be destroyed, no further Matrix transfer or creation will be possible. The remaining Eternals may continue living for thousands of years, but every subsequent death will become permanent. Their population will only decline, and the species will eventually become extinct.

The famous Eternal [[Ajak]] carries a Shepherd Matrix.

## Cosmic Channeling

**Cosmic Channeling** is the conscious activation of a Celestial Matrix.

The name does not refer to energy drawn specifically from outer space. Early Eternals understood the natural energy permeating the land, atmosphere, [[World Tree]], sky islands, and moons as one continuous cosmic medium.

All Eternals can direct additional Aura into the universal functions of their Matrix. They can strengthen their passive bodily reinforcement, accelerate regeneration, increase resistance to hostile environments, and release raw Aura as heat, light, or concussive force.

However, an Eternal’s most efficient form of Cosmic Channeling is determined by their Matrix design.

An Arsenal Matrix bearer can theoretically learn an Emission-based [[Hatsu]] and fire projectiles, but their Matrix is naturally optimized for stable energy constructs. A Dominion Matrix bearer can train their body through ordinary Nen, but their artificial racial system remains specialized for influencing minds.

Cosmic Channeling does not allow an Eternal to perform the exclusive functions of another Matrix design. No amount of ordinary training allows a non-Shepherd Eternal to extract a Matrix, create a Matrix Seed, implant a Matrix into a Human, repair a Matrix as an independent organ, or guide the construction of a new Matrix.

Those functions depend upon biological structures and Celestial permissions found only in the Shepherd Matrix.

## Creation and Succession

Eternals cannot reproduce naturally.

Their bodies retain the outward reproductive anatomy inherited from Humanity, but the Celestial Matrix prevents the production of viable offspring. The Eternal Form treats the creation of genetically independent reproductive cells as an unacceptable deviation from its stored pattern, correcting or destroying them before conception can occur.

This sterility was built into the original Angels design.

The Angels did not intend for Eternals to become an uncontrolled natural population. Every Eternal was meant to be constructed deliberately, assigned a Matrix design, and incorporated into a supervised community. Their number, purpose, and distribution could therefore be controlled without relying upon birth, inheritance, or unpredictable biological variation.

A new Eternal can be produced in only two ways: the construction of a new Matrix or the succession of an existing Matrix.

Both procedures require the direct involvement of a living Shepherd Matrix bearer.

### Matrix Creation

The first method is the construction of an entirely new Celestial Matrix.

A new Matrix must be grown from Aura-conducting tissue, given a stable Eternal Form, assigned a specialized design, and prepared to bind itself to a living soul. The completed Matrix is implanted into a Human host, after which it spreads gradually through the nervous system, blood vessels, organs, muscles, bones, and skin.

The transformation does not erase the Human’s mind. The host retains their memories, personality, identity, and existing Nen knowledge. Their body is gradually reconstructed according to the Matrix’s Eternal Form until they become biologically Eternal.

Only a Shepherd can complete this process.

The Shepherd first awakens the unfinished Matrix and establishes communication with its developing Aura network. They then guide the Matrix into the host, prevent it from treating the Human body as foreign matter, and mediate the initial connection between the Matrix and the host’s soul.

Once that connection is stable, the Shepherd directs the Matrix as it maps the host’s body, constructs an Eternal Form, and begins replacing ordinary Human limitations with Eternal physiology.

The process is extremely dangerous. An improperly formed Matrix may reject the host, tear apart their Aura pathways, produce uncontrolled regeneration, or reconstruct the body without successfully binding itself to the soul.

A Shepherd must continually balance the identity of the Human host against the corrective instincts of the Matrix. If the Matrix becomes dominant, it may reduce the host to a living body without a coherent self. If the host rejects it too strongly, both may die.

A Forge Matrix bearer may be needed to reconstruct the necessary apparatus. A powerful energy source must sustain the growing Matrix. A complete and undamaged design template must be available. A suitable Human host must survive the transformation. Above all, a skilled Shepherd must oversee every stage.

The successful creation of a new Matrix permanently increases the total number of Eternals by one. Such an event is exceptionally rare and may require years of preparation by an entire Eternal community.

Every failed attempt risks killing the Human host, destroying irreplaceable Celestial material, and weakening or permanently damaging the Shepherd performing the procedure.

### Matrix Succession

The second method is the transfer of an existing Matrix from one host to another.

Although the Matrix normally permeates the entire body, a Shepherd can separate it from the bearer and condense it into a temporary structure called a **Matrix Seed**. The Seed contains the complete Matrix design, the Eternal Form, its compatibility with the Uni-Mind, and the specialised racial ability carried by that Matrix.

The Shepherd must first place the existing bearer into a state of complete Matrix stillness. They then distinguish the bearer’s soul from the artificial structures woven throughout the body and gradually withdraw those structures without allowing the Matrix to collapse.

The Matrix cannot perform this extraction upon itself. Its own regulatory instincts interpret separation from the bearer as catastrophic bodily destruction and immediately attempt to resist or regenerate. Only the Shepherd Matrix can suppress those responses while keeping the extracted system alive.

Once condensed, the Matrix Seed can be preserved temporarily outside a body. It must then be implanted into a Human host under the guidance of the same Shepherd or another Shepherd capable of reading its design.

The transferred Matrix spreads through the new host and reconstructs them as an Eternal.

A transfer does not create an additional Eternal. It replaces one bearer with another.

The previous bearer permanently loses the Matrix. Their agelessness, regeneration, environmental resistance, and specialised ability disappear with it. If the bearer has lived beyond the natural limits of their underlying Human body, accumulated age and damage begin asserting themselves almost immediately.

For most ancient Eternals, surrendering the Matrix is fatal.

A younger Eternal may survive and return to something resembling an ordinary Human condition, but this is uncommon. The procedure was designed as a form of succession, not safe retirement.

The new bearer does not become the previous bearer reincarnated. The Matrix preserves its design and bodily pattern, not the former Eternal’s complete soul or personality.

However, very old Matrices often retain residual impressions from previous bearers: instincts, emotional associations, fragments of sensory memory, reflexive responses, or an unconscious familiarity with abilities the new host has never used.

These impressions can create a strong sense of continuity between bearers without making them the same person.

Because Matrix succession preserves rather than increases the total number of Eternals, the species’ population remained relatively consistent for much of the Ancient Era. Succession changed who carried each surviving Matrix but did not add to their number.

Deaths that destroy the Matrix permanently reduce the Eternal population. Only successful Matrix creation can reverse such losses.

Since the disappearance of the Celestials, the number of Eternals has therefore fluctuated within a narrow and generally declining range. Rare new Matrices have occasionally replaced losses, but their creation has never become reliable enough to permit significant population growth.

As a result, every Celestial Matrix is treated as an irreplaceable historical object as much as a biological organ. Some have passed through many hosts and bear names older than their current wielders. Others have remained with the same Eternal since the Ancient Era.

Shepherd Matrices are guarded above all others. Their bearers are not merely healers or leaders; they are the only living means by which an Eternal lineage can continue after its current bearer dies.

## Risk of Extinction

The survival of the Eternal species depends upon the continued existence of the Shepherd Matrices.

So long as at least one Shepherd survives, damaged Matrices can potentially be repaired, existing Matrices can be transferred to new bearers, and under exceptional circumstances entirely new Matrices can be created.

Should every Shepherd Matrix be destroyed, the species will lose all means of succession.

An Eternal who willingly attempts to surrender their Matrix will be unable to separate it from their body. A Matrix recovered from a dead Eternal cannot be safely stabilized or implanted. An unfinished Matrix cannot be awakened or bound to a soul. Even intact design archives will become useless because no other Matrix can perform the necessary biological and spiritual operations.

The remaining Eternals will still be ageless and extraordinarily difficult to kill. Their extinction may therefore take thousands or even tens of thousands of years.

Nevertheless, it will become inevitable.

Every Eternal destroyed after the death of the last Shepherd will represent the permanent disappearance of one more Matrix. No successors will replace them, no new lineages will be established, and no damaged Matrix can be restored after catastrophic failure.

The final Eternal may survive long after every civilization that knew the species has vanished, but once that last Matrix is destroyed, the Eternals will cease to exist completely.

## Descendants

Eternals have no biological descendants.

When Eternals speak of their descendants, they mean the Humans chosen to inherit their Matrices, the later bearers of a particular Matrix line, or the peoples and civilizations shaped by their actions.

An Eternal lineage is therefore not a bloodline. It is a chain of succession maintained by the Shepherds.

A Matrix may pass from teacher to student, guardian to chosen heir, ruler to successor, or parent-like figure to an adopted child. Different Eternal communities developed their own laws governing who may inherit a Matrix, whether its current bearer may refuse succession, and who possesses the authority to approve a new host.

Regardless of local custom, no succession can occur without a Shepherd.

The current bearer may choose an heir, a council may approve the candidate, and a Forge bearer may prepare the necessary machinery, but only a Shepherd can remove the Matrix, preserve it as a Seed, and implant it into the successor.

Newly created Matrices establish new lineages. Transferred Matrices continue existing ones.

The oldest Eternal lineages can trace every bearer back to the Human into whom the Celestials first implanted the original Matrix. Some lineages preserve detailed records of every succession, while others have lost portions of their history and rely upon the residual memories carried within the Matrix itself.

A lineage ends permanently if its Matrix is destroyed or if its bearer dies after the loss of every Shepherd capable of facilitating succession.
