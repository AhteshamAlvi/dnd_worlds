A **Mutant** is an individual occurrence, not an inherited classification. What matters is not how rare the alteration is or whether anyone else has it — it is that this happened to *this person*. Somebody on the far side of the world developing the identical condition changes nothing: they are a second instance, not a fellow member of anything. See [[Classification]] for how this tier sits against [[0 Clans|Clan]], [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtype]], and species.

The alteration may be spontaneous, externally triggered, or the result of surviving some transformation. It may also be **heritable**, and often is — that does not retroactively make the original person something other than a Mutant. Once a Mutant has descendants who reliably inherit the trait, those descendants can become a [[0 Clans|Clan]]; enough independent lineages carrying it, and the trait can eventually become a [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtype]]. The ancestor at the bottom of that ladder stays a Mutant the whole way up.

This is why several unrelated **Dragon Slayers** are all Mutants even when their abilities are indistinguishable. They are separate instances of the same possible alteration rather than members of one biological population. The same holds for [[Quincy]], [[Fullbringer|Fullbringers]], [[Benders|Kinetics]], **Heavenly Restrictions**, and anyone turned into a [[Vampires|Vampire]].

## Causes

The causes are as varied as the chance behind them, and plenty have no discoverable cause at all:

- **Anomalous birth** — born different to two entirely ordinary parents, no explanation required. A person born with four arms, two mouths, and four eyes is a Mutant in exactly this sense: nothing in the bloodline predicted it, nothing repeated it.
- **Exposure** — eating a rare mineral, drinking contaminated water, handling a strange relic, being caught in a burst of raw aura or emotion far stronger than a body was built to survive.
- **Place** — falling into a strange lake, wandering into land warped by centuries of aura, curse, or grief soaked into the ground.
- **Survived transformation** — a change inflicted deliberately or by accident and lived through: infection, sealing, remaking, ignition.
- **No cause at all** — some people are just born different, and no one, including them, ever finds out why.

## Not always an addition

A Mutation doesn't have to grant anything. Sometimes it's an absence instead — and the starkest example in this world is being born **completely unable to use Nen** (like [[Maki Zenin]], or [[Asta]]) No aura nodes will ever open; no teacher, no technique, no amount of training will ever change it. Because virtually every human here is born with the innate capacity to become a Nen user, being entirely closed off from it is just as rare, and just as much a Mutation, as growing a second pair of arms. People born this way are often pushed to compensate — driving physical conditioning, weapon skill, or raw non-Nen craft far past anything a Nen user would ever need to bother with, because it's the only path left open to them, and some become monstrously good at exactly that.

## Range

Mutations can be as visible as an extra limb or as invisible as a missing sense — physical, internal, cosmetic, functional, anywhere in between. Most stop with the person they happened to. Where one does prove stable enough to breed true, the *descendants* become a [[0 Clans|Clan]], and if that Clan's trait later spreads across enough independent lineages to stop being any one family's, it becomes a [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtype]]. None of that reaches back and reclassifies the person it started with.
