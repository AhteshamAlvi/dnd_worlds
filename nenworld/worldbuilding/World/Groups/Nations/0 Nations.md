A Nation is any sovereign polity: a kingdom, an empire, a republic, a hidden village, a city-state, an island country, or in some verses an entire world or realm that answers to no one above it. The list below is the working roster drawn from the fifteen source verses; it exists to be pruned, merged, and mapped onto [[The World of Sorane]] rather than imported wholesale.
