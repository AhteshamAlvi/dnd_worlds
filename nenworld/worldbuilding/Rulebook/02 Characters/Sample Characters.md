## Tessa Korrin — human, Street Kid → Transmuter duelist

*Concept:* pickpocket who saw a licensed duelist stop a riot with a raised hand, and hasn't slept right since. *Goal:* be untouchable. *Flaw:* can't resist an audience. *Ties:* kid sister (warm), fence who owns her debt (barbed).

**Creation (Tier 0).** Array + human (+1 AGI, +1 SPI, extra pick):
STR 9 (−1) · AGI 16 (+3) · DEX 14 (+2) · CON 13 (+1) · VIT 10 · INT 8 (−1) · WIS 8 (−1) · PER 12 (+1) · CHA 11 · SPI 11
**Body HP 30** (VIT 10×3) · **EVA 13** · **SR 2** (AGI 14+) · **Initiative +1** (PER) · Reflex save prof
Skills: Stealth, Streetwise, Sleight of Hand (background) · Martial Arts (formative: "the riot") · Acrobatics, Perception (picks) · Insight (human pick)
Gear: knife (1d6), lockpicks, 200 ryn. Aura block: **blank**.
*In play she's a scout and second-story artist. A militia thug (P 12) is a real fight. Her CON 13 is invisible now — it's the deep aura ceiling she'll grow into; her DEX 14 will place every strike and shape every future construct.*

**End of arc 1 (~14 weeks, crisis Initiation in session 3).**
On awakening she has her full **CON-13/VIT-10 pool: max AP 30** (derived, [[Aura Mathematics]]). The first arc is two fresh-nodes CON breakthroughs (→ **CON 15, AP 60**) and the climb to Ren IV. Principles: Ten IV, Zetsu II, Ren IV, Hatsu II. Physiological Output Capacity **100** (CON 15) × Ren IV access 40% = ceiling 40, and that ceiling binds before her 60-point pool does: **AO 40** · Control 1 (×2) · Regen G 1/hr, resting 0.5/hr (VIT 10, unchanged all arc) · Ten shroud (rank IV, 1% of pool) Guard ~1, Soak 0 — a raincoat, not armor.
Divination (GM's sealed roll): **Transmuter.** The water tasted of copper and rain. *(The reveal was a [[Nen Manifestation|Water Divination]] scene, not a die she controlled.)*
*Fights now: commits her full 40 AO to a knife strike — Strike aura 40 × 100% (in-type) + Physical Force (knife 3.5 × STR 9's ×0.84) ≈ **P 43**. DEX +2 lands it; a working professional's Guard barely notices it. Her pool is deep for her band; her* output *(Ren) is the constraint. She has started noticing her aura goes* sharp *when she's showing off — the Seed.*

**Year two (professional).** **CON 17, VIT 10 → max AP 100** · Physiological Output 600, Ren IV (40%) ceiling 240 — pool-bound again: **AO 100** (Current Aura, not CON or Ren, is her cap now) · Control 2 (×1.6) · Gyō IV, In II, Ken II · Hatsu IV (budget 20). Four points of CON in a year of hard training — she is becoming faintly superhuman.
**Ability — "Static Veil"** (TRA 100%): aura charged to biting static. *Seating it was her first SPI manifestation roll ([[Nen Manifestation]]) — she made it on the second try.* Build: minor rider (numbing) 2 EP + In-woven 3 EP = 5 EP. Condition: only conducts through metal she's holding (Major +5 → banked). In play: commits 70 of her 100 AO to the strike (holding the rest back for Guard, the professional habit Combat XP season's punishing) — knife + 70 Strike × 1.0 (in-type) + Physical Force (knife 3.5 × STR 9's ×0.84) ≈ **P 73**; accuracy d20 + DEX 2 + Martial; hidden wind-up (her In II / DEX vs their PER / Gyō); victims save Fortitude DC 10 + DEX 2 + Hatsu 2 = **14** or Numbed.
*She duels professionals as an equal and reads two bands up — and has learned exactly one humiliating lesson about a Guard she couldn't crack.*

## Brakk Ur-Denna — Orckin, Soldier → Enhancer wall

*Concept:* discharged line-breaker who swore to a dead captain he'd never let a line break behind him again. *Goal:* a company of his own. *Flaw:* keeps promises past all sense. *Ties:* the captain's daughter (warm), his old quartermaster who sells to both sides (barbed).

**Creation.** Array + Orckin (STR +2, CON +1, VIT +1; Thick Hide +1 natural Soak; Oathbound; Enhancement leaning) ([[Peoples (Races)]]):
STR 17 (+3) · AGI 10 · DEX 8 (−1) · CON 15 (+2) · VIT 14 (+2) · INT 8 (−1) · WIS 11 · PER 12 (+1) · CHA 9 (−1) · SPI 10
**Body HP 42** (VIT 14×3) · **EVA 10** · **SR 2** (Athletics from formative) · **Initiative +1** (PER) · Soak 1 (hide) +2 (old heavy armor, −1 EVA) · Fortitude (VIT) save prof
Skills: Weapons (hafted), Perception, Endurance (background) · Athletics (formative: "the last stand at the ford") · Grappling, Composure (picks)
Gear: war maul (2d8), battered heavy armor, service medallion, 200 ryn.
*At creation he's the party's actual wall — Soak 3 shrugs knives (P ≤ 3 does nothing). His CON 15 quietly promises the deepest aura pool of the three; the fodder phase is his to shine in.*

**End of arc 1 (~12 weeks, militia meditation program, competent teacher).**
On awakening he has his **CON-15/VIT-14 pool: max AP 300** (Orckin constitution runs deep). A militia meditation program + a teacher raise him to **CON 16 (AP 500)** across the first arc. Ten IV, Zetsu II, Ren II, Hatsu II. Physiological Output 200 (CON 16), Ren II access 20% → ceiling 40, well under his pool: **AO 40** · Control 1 · Regen G 20/hr, resting 10/hr (VIT 14 — he tops off fast between fights).
Divination: **Enhancer** (the leaning landed; the glass overflowed and cracked).
*Ren II — below Ren IV, so per the Allocation rules he still fights crude: all-Strike or all-Guard, nothing split. All-in on a maul swing: Strike aura 40 × 100% (in-type) + Physical Force (maul 2d8 avg 9 × STR 17's ×3.36) ≈ **P 70**. His Guard (the alternate all-in round) + armor: front-line real. He learned Ten fastest in his cohort — Orckin discipline is built for it, and Ten is now his containment stat for every future Kō.*

**Year two.** **CON 18, VIT 14 → max AP 1k** (his high starting CON compounds fastest — the tank has the deepest reserves) · Physiological Output 1k, Ren IV (40%) → ceiling 400: **AO 400** · Control 2 · Chū IV (Enhancer k=2 — reaches its +4 rank cap at low density), Ken II · Hatsu IV.
**Ability — "The Held Line"** (ENH 100%): his Guard extends to ground he's sworn to hold. Build: barrier channel 3 EP + project Guard onto ally 4 EP = 7 EP. Condition: only while a living ally is behind him (Major +5). Vow: *"The line breaks through me first"* — Serious ×1.5 on Guard while anyone shelters behind it (audited: yes, it will kill him one day).
In play: commits 300 of his 400 AO to Guard, even spread → Guard 300 × 1.25 (channel) × 1.5 (vow) ≈ **563, whole-body Soak 56**, and he can lend half to the medic behind him. Chū poured whole-body (density ~1) gives a modest even boost; concentrated into his maul arm it tempers STR far higher (the density table, [[Aura Density and Concentration]]).
*He is now what he swore to be. The GM owes his vow a terrible night.*

## Neru Vall — Elf, Scholar → Conjurer investigator

*Concept:* archive-elf who outlived the case that mattered and reopened it. *Goal:* the truth of the Veyra fire. *Flaw:* treats people like documents — misfiled ones. *Ties:* a human partner aging fast (warm), the archivist who sealed the file (barbed).

**Creation.** Array + Elf (AGI +1, WIS +1, SPI +1; Inborn Attunement: awakening programs halved, Aura Sense usable pre-awakening; long-lived) ([[Peoples (Races)]]):
STR 8 (−1) · AGI 11 · DEX 14 (+2) · CON 12 (+1) · VIT 8 (−1) · INT 15 (+2) · WIS 14 (+2) · PER 10 · CHA 9 (−1) · SPI 12 (+1)
**Body HP 24** (VIT 8×3) · **EVA 10** · **SR 1** · **Initiative +0** (PER) · Spirit save prof
Skills: Academics, Nen Theory *(now WIS — understanding Nen)*, Investigation (background) · Medicine (formative: "the fire's survivors") · Perception, Aura Sense (picks — the elf trait makes the second legal pre-awakening)
Gear: case files, mentor's letter, plain robes, 200 ryn.
*Fragile as paper (Body 24 — a pistol is a death sentence) and already the party's eyes: passive Aura Sense 10 + PER + prof before anyone else can feel anything. Her DEX 14 is the real Conjurer engine — precise aura shaping — and her CON 12 a respectable future pool despite the frail body.*

**End of arc 1 (~10 weeks, self-guided meditation, halved by trait).**
On awakening she has her **CON-12/VIT-8 pool: max AP 10.** Elf attunement speeds her CON breakthroughs; she reaches **CON 14 (AP 20)** by arc's end. Ten II, Zetsu II, Ren II, Hatsu II. Physiological Output 40 (CON 14), Ren II access 20% → ceiling 8, under her pool: **AO 8** · Control 1 · Regen G 0.3/hr, resting 0.15/hr (VIT 8 — her pool refills *slowly*; she rations it).
Divination: **Conjurer** — motes of ash appeared in the water. Neru wept, and didn't say why.
*Combat contribution: staying alive (Guard all-in even, EVA 10, run). Investigative contribution: everything. This is a build the party protects because it aims them.*

**Year two.** **CON 16, VIT 8 → max AP 40** · Physiological Output 200, Ren IV (40%) → ceiling 80 — but her 40-point pool binds first: **AO 40** (pool-bound, not Ren-bound — her low VIT has caught up with her) · Control 3 (drilled daily — archivists are patient) · Gyō IV, Shū II · Hatsu IV.
**Ability — "Card Catalogue"** (CONJ 100%): conjured index cards that record what Neru has studied. *Its manifestation leaned on her SPI 12 — a Conjurer's constructs are seated by talent ([[Nen Manifestation]]) and shaped by DEX.* Build: detect one thing — "does this face/object match a filed card?" — 3 EP + Independence (3−1) 2 EP + scene duration (5−2) 3 EP = 8 EP. Conditions: a card must be filed by touching subject or true document for a full minute (Major +5); cards burn if Neru lies about their contents (Major toll +5).
In play: a conjured, *visible*, legally admissible archive ([[Conjuration]]: real to non-users) — courts love him, criminals want him dead.
*Growth trigger he's chasing: filing a card on someone who no longer exists (→ forensic echo form, 9 EP, waiting in budget).*

---

**What the three show together:** identical creation budgets diverge into scout/wall/eyes *before* Nen; **CON/VIT placement (13/10, 15/14, 12/8)** sets three different starting pools — and, because pool is derived from CON and VIT together, three different *growth spines* (raise either, raise the pool), which is why the Orckin tank's high-VIT start ends year two with the deepest reserves and the fastest regen; **DEX (14/8/14)** decides who executes precise Nen and who swings blunt force; **SPI (11/10/12)** sets whose manifestations land first try. Each ability grew from the sheet's ties and flaws — the [[Hatsu Design]] Seed test passing in the wild. At year two all three sit in the professional band together despite different pools (100/1k/40) and CON (17/18/16): bands are wide, parties hold together ([[Balance and Math]]).
