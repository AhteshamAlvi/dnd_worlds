**Race:** Human — last daughter of the mage-city of Belialuin

**Nen Type:** [[Specialization|Specialist]]
![[specialization.png]]
Infinity is a single impossible law — "what I set does not end" — applied to her own work. That is a Specialist's shape rather than any elemental category. The enormous *breadth* of her ordinary spellwork is scholarship and study on top of it: she is the most learned Nen theorist in the setting, which reads mechanically as very high Nen Theory rather than as more categories.

**Nen Ability:** Infinity ([[Specialization]])
- Anything she casts stays exactly where she set it, forever, unless she chooses to release it. Effects do not expire, decay, or run down.

## Infinity

The consequences are broader than they first sound. A spell held mid-effect stays mid-effect; a thing frozen stays frozen; a container she seals stays sealed indefinitely. It also means her preparation compounds over centuries — every working she has ever left running is still running.

## Scholarship

Her real advantage in most confrontations is that she has already studied the thing she is fighting. She collects, catalogues, and experiments on abilities and artifacts, and enters fights having pre-solved them. That is scholarship and preparation, not a power.
