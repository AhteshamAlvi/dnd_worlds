**Race:** Human — made immortal by the Fountain of Youth

**Nen Type:** [[Manipulation|Manipulator]]
![[manipulation.png]]
Snatch takes hold of something at a distance and moves it, or takes a property off it and keeps it. Both halves are control exerted over a target that isn't his, which is Manipulation's core. His immortality is not an ability at all — it came from drinking at the Fountain of Youth, so it is an artifact effect layered on top of his own Nen.

**Nen Ability:** Snatch ([[Manipulation]])
- Steals what belongs to something else — physical objects, a body's freedom of movement, or a quality like strength itself — and takes it at range.

## Snatch

The ability has several expressions of the same idea:
- **Hunt** — pulls objects, or people, toward him from a distance.
- **Wire** — binds and restricts a target's movement.
- **Banish** — throws with the same grip in reverse.
- **Fox Hunt** — the direct theft of physical strength from a target, adding it to his own.

## Immortality

Not [[0 Nen|Nen]]. The Fountain of Youth left him unable to die: wounds close, severed parts reattach, and age stopped. It is a permanent property of his body, which is why it persists even when his aura is spent — and why it can only be countered by something that removes the effect rather than by out-damaging him.
