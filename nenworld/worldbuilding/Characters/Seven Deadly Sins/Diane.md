**Race:** [[Giant]]

**Nen Type:** [[Manipulation|Manipulator]]
![[manipulation.png]]
Creation commands earth, sand, and stone that already exist rather than making new matter — she tells the ground what to do and it obeys. That is Manipulation of non-living material, not [[Conjuration]]. Her physical output is mostly racial: a [[Giant]]'s Scale Factor does the heavy lifting before any aura is spent.

**Nen Ability:** Creation ([[Manipulation]])
- Commands earth and stone, raising it into walls, spears, hammers, and moving terrain.

## Creation

Standard expressions include hurling compressed earth as projectiles, raising walls and pits to reshape a battlefield, and driving spikes up from underfoot. Because it works on material already present, it is devastating on open ground and much weaker on stone floors, water, or anywhere the earth has been sealed away from her.

## Giant physiology

Her size is the other half of every fight she is in. See [[Giant]] for what the race brings on its own — reach, mass, and durability that need no aura at all.
