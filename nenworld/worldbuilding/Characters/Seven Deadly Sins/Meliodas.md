**Race:** [[Demon]] — former leader of the Ten Commandments

**Nen Type:** [[Specialization|Specialist]]
![[specialization.png]]
Full Counter is a conditional reversal that no ordinary category reproduces: it does not block, absorb, or out-damage an attack, it *returns* it amplified. That "one impossible rule, absolutely obeyed" shape is Specialization. His baseline demon aura (darkness, flight, raw physical output) runs underneath it as ordinary Enhancement-flavored output, not as the ability itself.

**Nen Ability:** Full Counter ([[Specialization]])
- Any attack directed at him is reflected back at the attacker with greater force than it was thrown.

## Full Counter

The reversal is not a choice made after the fact — it is a standing property of his aura, which is why it triggers on attacks he never saw coming. The [[Conditions|Condition]] that makes it possible is also its exact weakness: it answers *incoming attacks*, so an opponent who never attacks him is never countered, and effects that are not "an attack directed at him" pass straight through the rule.

## Demon Mark

Releasing his sealed demon power floods the body with black aura, restoring the raw output of a Commandment-tier demon. Treated as a separate, escalating state rather than a second ability.

## Assault Mode

The full, unsealed form — the state he is imprisoned in his own history for. Power at this tier is less an ability than a magnitude problem for everyone standing nearby.
