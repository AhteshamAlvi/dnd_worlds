The root note of this vault. Everything else hangs off one of the three trunks below.

This vault holds two related things. The first is a **setting**: a "real world +" world that takes fifteen-plus anime source verses and fuses them into a single coherent place with one shared history, one shared biology, and one shared power system — [[0 Nen|Nen]]. The second is **Nenworld**, a standalone d20 tabletop RPG built on that setting, which turns the lore into playable math. The lore comes first and is never overridden by the rules; where the rules quantify something the lore left qualitative, they say so.

**The four things that make this system what it is.** Everyone starts as ordinary — no Nen, no abilities — and awakens in play. Power differences are real orders of magnitude, not tier labels, so a party that deals 30 damage simply cannot hurt something with Soak 400. Accuracy stays bounded (roughly −2 to +14 forever) while magnitude lives in damage multipliers, Guard, Soak, and aura pools written in k/M notation. And body durability (**Body HP**) is separate from aura protection (**Guard**), with attacks resolving as accuracy → penetration → damage.

### World — the lore

Canon. The Rulebook builds on these pages and never contradicts them. Hub: [[0 World|World Building]].

- **[[0 Power System|Power System]]** — [[0 Nen|Nen]] is the spine of the setting: [[Aura]], [[The Nen Path]], the [[0 Four Major Principles|Four Major Principles]] ([[Ten]], [[Zetsu]], [[Ren]], [[Hatsu]]), the [[0 Intermediate Principles|Intermediate Principles]] and [[0 Advanced Principles|Advanced Principles]], the six [[0 Types of Nen|Types of Nen]] and [[Water Divination]], [[Nen Ability|Nen Abilities]] and [[Developing a Nen Ability|how they are developed]], and the cost side of it all in [[0 Nen and Limitation|Nen and Limitation]] ([[Conditions]], [[Vows and Limitations]], [[Risk]]). Also [[0 Learning Nen|Learning Nen]], [[Nen and Nature]], [[Nen in Society]], and the system-light [[Nen at the Table]]. Separately: [[Devil's Fruits]].
- **[[0 Races|Race]]** — nobody is gatekept from Nen by species. [[0 Origin Species|Origin Species]] traces every humanoid people back to one ancestor, the [[Crown Hominid]], split into the [[Ancient Giants]], [[Devils]], and [[Angels]], converging again in [[The Origin of Humans]]. [[0 Secondary Species|Secondary Species]] holds the lineages in between — the descendants that died out or became something else. [[0 Current Species|Current Species]] holds everyone alive today — [[Humans]], [[Beastfolk]], [[Elf|Elves]], [[Giant|Giants]], [[Merfolk]], [[Saiyan|Saiyans]], [[Spirits]], [[Vampires]], [[Hollow|Hollows]], [[Monsters]] and more. [[Classification]] is the standard that sorts all of it, and the tiers below the species line — a trait that landed on one person is a [[0 Mutants|Mutation]], a trait a family keeps is a [[0 Clans|Clan]], a trait a whole population is born with is a [[0 Racial Subtypes|Racial Subtype]] — carry most of the documented variation inside [[Humans|Humanity]].
- **Groups** — [[0 Nations|Nations]] (sovereign polities: kingdoms, empires, hidden villages, sky islands) and [[0 Organizations|Organizations]] (anything you join rather than are born into: guilds, corps, crews, conspiracies). Both are working rosters drawn from the source verses, meant to be pruned and mapped onto [[The World of Sorane]] rather than imported wholesale.

## Vault conventions

- **Every note breadcrumbs home.** The second line of a note is its trail: `[[0 Index|Index]] › [[0 World|World Building]] › [[0 Races|Race]]` for lore, `[[0 Rulebook|Rulebook]] › Section` for rules.
- **Lore and design stay distinguishable.** Anything invented for balance is labelled as a system-design addition, with its reasoning. Where the lore gives a number (the 100/80/60/40 [[0 Types of Nen|type affinities]], [[En]]'s 2 m minimum, [[Ken]] ≈ 10× [[Ten]]), the rules reproduce it exactly.
- **Links resolve by basename**, so note titles are unique across the vault; the one collision (*Progression and Training*, which exists in both [[0 Learning Nen|Learning Nen]] and the Rulebook) is linked by full path. Inside table cells, alias pipes are escaped as `\|`.
- **Magnitudes** use k/M suffixes at two significant figures — `8.4k`, `250k`.
- **Terminology is identical everywhere.** Notably Conjuration's category code is **CONJ**, never CON — CON is Constitution.
- Wiki-link only to notes that exist; bold anything that is a concept without a note of its own.

## Known soft spots

Worth knowing before you trust a page end to end:

- [[The World of Sorane]] and every number in [[Canon Benchmarks]] are proposals, not settled lore — rename, redraw, or veto freely.
- [[0 Origin Species|Origin Species]] was written from a newer account of the world's history and contradicts several older notes under [[0 Current Species|Current Species]] — [[Giant]], [[Sky People]], and [[Fairy]] each still tell their own origin story. Reconciling them is pending. (The Demon and Goddess notes, which conflicted hardest, have since been removed.)
- Three source anomalies are flagged rather than fixed: [[Sein]]'s file reads like a [[Gojo Satoru]] paste, the Blessed section of [[Spirits]] carries a stray Cursed Spirit sentence, and [[Asta]] is written as both "lacks all Nen" and a Specialist (read as mutation-plus-artifact, see [[Canon Benchmarks]]).
- Several races are lore stubs with only generic rules coverage via [[Race Rules Overview]]: [[Dragon]], [[Dwarf]], [[Namekian]] under [[0 Current Species|Current Species]], and all three entries under [[0 Secondary Species|Secondary Species]] ([[Buccaneer]], [[Lunarian]], [[Tuffle]]) — which is also why those three are filed there provisionally.
